# Converting HTML One-Page Templates to WordPress and Shopify Themes: Go/No-Go Evidence (2025–2026)

Method note: WebFetch could not reach any outside host in this session (shopify.dev, therepository.email and searchenginejournal.com all failed DNS because they are outside the allowed network). Every finding below comes from search-result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. Treat exact numbers as "reported by the source" and check them on the primary page before the final decision. The research date is November 2026.

## Q1. WordPress: market size and trend (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
WordPress is still the largest CMS, with about 40% of all websites and about 58–60% of the known-CMS market. But its share fell in 2026 for the first time in its history, and Shopify is the platform gaining share. The market is huge but slowly shrinking, not growing.

### Cited Findings
- SEJ, citing W3Techs data from November 13, 2026, puts WordPress at a 57.9% share of the CMS market. "Usage" means share of all sites and "market share" means share of sites with a known CMS — [Search Engine Journal, Nov 2026](https://searchenginejournal.com/cms-market-share-trends-top-content-management-systems-nov-2026/596729)
- October 2026 reports give about 40.1–40.2% of all websites and 58.7% of known-CMS sites — [Colorlib CMS market share](https://colorlib.com/wp/cms-market-share/); [Barn2](https://barn2.com/wordpress-market-share/)
- Shopify rose from 6.8% to 7.9% between Nov 2025 and Nov 2026. In 2026 the gains went to Shopify, while Wix and Squarespace levelled off — [SEJ Nov 2026](https://searchenginejournal.com/cms-market-share-trends-top-content-management-systems-nov-2026/596729)
- WordPress's share grew every year from 2003 to 2024 and fell for the first time in 2026. The decline sped up from December 2025 — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/wordpress-market-share/475075/); [dev.to/UnfoldCMS (vendor-authored, flag bias)](https://dev.to/hamed_pakdaman_c724e294d9/wordpress-market-share-declining-2026-data-3k8g)
- One source says known-CMS share fell from 65.2% in 2023 to 60.2% in Q1 2026. Its 2023 baseline conflicts with other sources, and figures earlier in 2026 range from 59.8% to 61.4% depending on the site — [hostingadvice](https://www.hostingadvice.com/studies/cms-market-share-statistics/)

### Inferences
- The addressable market is still very large. But "everyone uses WordPress" is weakening as a growth argument, and Shopify is the one platform with momentum.
- Small-business owners who would buy a one-page template are also the group Wix and Squarespace target, and those platforms do not sell third-party themes.

### Gaps
- I could not read W3Techs directly (network blocked), so the exact current figure is unconfirmed.

## Q2. ThemeForest / Envato Market: market state, Shutterstock/Getty, fee changes

### Takeaway
This is the single most important finding. From July 1, 2026, Envato removed the exclusive author model and moved every Market author to a flat 50% revenue share, citing "challenging market conditions" and buyers shifting to subscriptions (Elements). Commentators describe ThemeForest as declining for years, and Envato now sits inside a Shutterstock → Getty consolidation. A new author would enter a shrinking marketplace and keep about half of each sale.

### Cited Findings
- **Fee change:** From July 1, 2026, all ThemeForest and CodeCanyon authors (also AudioJungle, VideoHive and GraphicRiver) get a flat 50%. Before this, exclusive authors kept 50–87.5% depending on lifetime sales, and non-exclusive authors kept about 45% (a 55% fee) — [WP Gazette](https://wpgazette.com/industry-business/envato-50-revenue-share-exclusivity-ends/); [The Repository](https://www.therepository.email/envato-ends-exclusive-author-model-moves-all-marketplace-sellers-to-flat-50-revenue-share); [wp-content.co](https://wp-content.co/envato-ends-exclusive-author-model/)
- **Envato's stated reason:** "challenging market conditions", buyers moving to subscriptions and bundles, and a wish for one model aligned with Envato Elements — [wp-content.co](https://wp-content.co/envato-ends-exclusive-author-model/); [baewp](https://baewp.com/envato-ends-exclusive-author-model-shifts-all-sellers-flat/)
- **Worked example:** a $79 theme with a $12 buyer fee. A top exclusive author used to keep about $58.62 and now keeps about $33.50, roughly 43% less per sale — [WP Gazette](https://wpgazette.com/industry-business/envato-50-revenue-share-exclusivity-ends/)
- **Exclusivity:** exclusivity restrictions are reported as gone, so authors can now sell the same item elsewhere, including on their own site or Gumroad — [PureThemes](https://purethemes.net/envato-author-exclusivity-merchant-of-record/); [wpbiz.dev](https://wpbiz.dev/growth/envato-commission/)
- **Long-term decline (opinion):** "ThemeForest's traffic has reportedly been in decline since roughly 2015", and AI has sped this up (blogger opinion). Freemius founder Vova Feldman said ThemeForest and CodeCanyon "have been slowly dying for years" — [webstick.blog](https://webstick.blog/envato-scam); [The Repository](https://www.therepository.email/envato-ends-exclusive-author-model-moves-all-marketplace-sellers-to-flat-50-revenue-share). Flag: Freemius and PureThemes profit when authors leave Envato.
- **Ownership:** Shutterstock completed its acquisition of Envato on July 23, 2024 — [Shutterstock IR](https://investor.shutterstock.com/node/13786). Getty and Shutterstock announced a merger on Jan 7, 2025. The UK CMA Phase 1 found a "realistic prospect of a substantial lessening of competition", and a final report followed in May 2026. I could not confirm the outcome — [CMA phase 1 summary](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68f5fd0b1c9076042263f083/Summary_of_phase_1_decision.pdf); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/getty-images-acquires-shutterstock--3-7-billion-deal-dominate-stock-media)
- **Contributors under the merger:** a contributor submission to the CMA said suppliers had "no alternative platform of equivalent reach" and raised fears of worse commercial terms. This is one individual's view — [CMA contributor responses, Mar 2026](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69c67283cdfd19de13d0f794/Contributor_responses_to_the_interim_report_27_March_2026.pdf)
- **Industry narrative:** ThemeREX's 2026 piece covers changes among the top WordPress themes on ThemeForest (snippet only) — [ThemeREX](https://themerex.net/wp/themeforest-2026-top-wordpress-themes-what-changed/)

### Inferences
- A new author earns 50% either way, so exclusivity no longer matters. The seller can list on ThemeForest and keep selling on Gumroad at the same time.
- The fee cut came right after the parent company's consolidation, and it follows the stock-media pattern of falling prices and worse contributor terms. Further unfavourable changes are possible, though this is speculation.

### Gaps
- I found no official Envato figures on ThemeForest GMV, sales volume or Elements subscriber numbers for 2025–2026.
- I could not read the original Envato Author Hub announcement. Its date and terms come from secondary coverage, and some articles disagree on whether the change was upcoming or already in effect.

## Q3. ThemeForest review difficulty and technical requirements

### Takeaway
Hard rejections for design that is not "distinctive" or not up to the quality bar are the main risk. A rejection rate of about 70% circulates, but it has no source. ThemeForest WordPress themes must pass Envato's WP requirements (Theme Check-style rules, prefixing, no bundled unzipped plugins and so on). Practitioners report repeated soft rejections taking weeks to months.

### Cited Findings
- A claimed rejection rate of "around 70%" has no data behind it, so it is unverified — [ThemesCamp 2025 checklist](https://themescamp.com/wordpress-checklist-for-themeforest-as-an-author/)
- **Hard-rejection causes:** below the marketplace's aesthetic standard, not distinctive, or not meeting basic requirements. **Soft rejections** are fixable code issues such as invalid markup, outdated bundled plugins, unprefixed functions, or a plugin file left unzipped — [UPQODE](https://upqode.com/themeforest-rejections-types-reasons/); [hasinhayder GitHub checklist](https://github.com/hasinhayder/themeforest-wp-theme-approval-checklist); [themeim](https://themeim.com/how-to-get-your-theme-approved-on-themeforest/)
- **Historical anecdotes (2013–2015, flagged old):** one author needed "two months and a half" of soft rejections to release a WP theme. Another took "3+ months to produce and publish one WordPress theme". Another took "nearly one year and a half to start selling" — [Medium, Oleg](https://medium.com/@oleg_67082/thorny-way-to-sales-at-themeforest-net-ee5d370f4278); [Medium, semenovfx](https://medium.com/@semenovfx/a-story-of-themeforest-author-s-experiments-92821fc86d48)
- **Block themes vs classic:** classic themes are not being discontinued, per a 2026 report — [DreamHost 2026](https://www.dreamhost.com/blog/the-future-of-wordpress/). About 70% of new themes were classic in a 2021 sample (old) — [WPBakery](https://wpbakery.com/blog/70-percent-of-all-new-wordpress-themes-are-classic/). An unsourced 2026 claim says block themes rose from 12% to 34%; I would not rely on it — [yaohehe blog](https://yaohehe.github.io/2026-05-20-wordpress-block-theme-vs-classic-theme-the-complet-en.html)
- **Elementor:** Envato supports Template Kits for Elementor and Elementor Pro only — [Envato author help: How To Create A Template Kit](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038229332-How-To-Create-A-Template-Kit)
- **PHP and WP core:** WordPress 7.0 raised the minimum PHP to 7.4, dropping 7.2 and 7.3. WP 7.1 keeps 7.4. Recommended PHP is 8.3. No fixed schedule exists for future bumps, though a Trac ticket discusses moving to 8.x — [Make WP Core, Jan 2026](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/01/09/dropping-support-for-php-7-2-and-7-3/); [PHP support clarification, May 2026](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/05/22/php-support-clarification-2026/); [Trac #64494](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/64494)

### Inferences
- One-page templates are a crowded ThemeForest niche, so "distinctive design" is where hard rejection is most likely. Trilingual support (EN/ZH/JA or similar) could help an item stand out, provided it is delivered as WPML/Polylang-ready plus .pot translations.
- Maintenance burden: the seller must test each WP major release (about 2–3 a year), the PHP 8.x versions, and any bundled builder (Elementor) updates. Block themes avoid builder dependency but follow fast Gutenberg changes.

### Gaps
- I found no official Envato rejection statistics, and no current Envato review guidance specific to block themes vs. Elementor-dependent themes.
- I found no reliable estimate of hours needed to convert an HTML one-pager to a block theme. My own estimate from general knowledge is about 20–60 hours per template for a reviewer-ready theme (theme.json, patterns, templates, demo import, documentation, translation). This is unsourced.

## Q4. ThemeForest support obligations, pricing and sales counts

### Takeaway
Supported items carry 6 months of included support, which buyers can extend to 12. Authors must keep items working and secure. One-page restaurant WP themes sell for about $59–$129, and lifetime sales counts are mostly in the tens, with a standout at about 300.

### Cited Findings
- All supported items include 6 months of support from purchase, and buyers can extend this to a maximum of 12 months. Most authors can choose whether to offer support. Updates that keep an item "working as described and protected against major security concerns" apply to all items — [ThemeForest Item Support Policy](https://themeforest.net/page/item_support_policy); [Envato help](https://help.market.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/206171030-Item-Support-FAQ)
- Support upgrade pricing is reported as 37.5% of item price at purchase, 62.5% during the support period and 87.5% after expiry. These figures may be outdated — [Envato help](https://help.market.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/208191263)
- **Sales evidence (lifetime, search snippet, pairings uncertain):** "Lily | One Page Restaurant WordPress Theme" at $69 with 313 sales; a Foodex one-page WP theme at $129 with 31 sales; a Dinex one-page WP theme with 11 sales; HTML one-page restaurant templates at $59 with 32 and 17 sales — [ThemeForest search "restaurant onepage"](https://themeforest.net/search/restaurant%20onepage)
- **Historical new-author economics (old, flagged):** "from $1,426 generated sales you as a beginner author get $419.05" after taxes and fees — [ehandbook/Medium](https://ehandbook.com/after-making-116k-with-themeforest-im-dropping-it-here-s-why-8462561dd33b?gi=b533f4b07107). A 2015 WP Tavern story reported a first sale minutes after approval, with 6 sales at $43 on day one — [WP Tavern](https://wptavern.com/new-theme-development-company-makes-first-sale-minutes-after-being-approved-on-themeforest)

### Inferences
- **Rough year-1 model (inference):** a $59 theme nets about $29.50 at 50%, before US withholding if applicable. At 20–50 sales per theme per year, which matches the lifetime counts above, that is about $600–$1,500 per theme per year. Five themes would bring about $3k–$7.5k, minus support time. The 313-sale Lily outcome is the top case, not the norm.
- Support load: one-page buyers are often non-technical small business owners, so customization questions will be common. The policy lets authors exclude customization, but this is still significant time spent in a non-native language.

### Gaps
- I found no aggregate data on median sales for new WP one-page themes in 2025–2026.

## Q5. WordPress alternatives: WordPress.org freemium, own-store block themes, Elementor kits

### Takeaway
WordPress.org lists only free (GPL) themes and works as a funnel, not revenue. The usual model is a free "lite" theme there plus a paid pro version sold on your own store (Gumroad, EDD, Freemius or Lemon Squeezy). Elementor template kits are cheap ($15–$50) and mostly earn through Envato Elements' subscription pool. None of these has solid 2025–2026 revenue data.

### Cited Findings
- WordPress.org themes must be free, so it serves as top-of-funnel. Freemium (lite on .org, premium on your own site) is the standard model — [Dodo Payments 2026 guide](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/sell-wordpress-plugins-themes); [Easy Digital Downloads](https://easydigitaldownloads.com/blog/selling-wordpress-themes/)
- The free directory holds over 14,000 themes and practitioners call it "oversaturated". Example model: $59 × 10 sales a month ≈ $7k a year (vendor illustration, not data) — [cartmango](https://cartmango.com/how-to-sell-wordpress-themes/); [wplift 2025](https://wplift.com/best-place-to-sell-wordpress-themes/)
- WordPress.org review can be strict. One author was "rejected twice by the WordPress.org theme review" — [Dan Fisher](https://dan-fisher.dev/wordpress-org-theme-review-rejected-twice/)
- Block themes package and sell like any zip, and tiered or freemium pricing is suggested — [maxiblocks](https://maxiblocks.com/build-and-sell-block-theme-wordpress/)
- Elementor kits sell for about $15–$30 per kit (launch-era figure) or $24–50 (Freemius guide, vendor). Envato Elements costs about $129 a year for buyers (Core) — [WP Tavern](https://wptavern.com/envato-launches-template-kits-marketplace-for-elementor); [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/how-to-make-money-sell-elementor-templates-template-kits/); [theplusaddons 2026](https://theplusaddons.com/blog/best-elementor-templates-resources/)
- Elementor kits are also sold on Gumroad, which suggests a lower-friction channel the seller already uses — [example Gumroad listing](https://digitalseo360.gumroad.com/l/colabora-creative-digital-agency-elementor-template-kit)

### Inferences
- **Lowest-risk WP path:** convert 3–5 templates into block themes (or Elementor kits) and sell them on the seller's existing Gumroad store alongside the HTML versions, as an "HTML + WP" upsell tier. This needs no marketplace review, no 50% cut, and no mandatory 6-month support. Optionally, list one "lite" theme on WordPress.org as a funnel.
- Elementor kits are faster to build (no PHP theme code) but depend on Elementor (Pro) and pay little per unit.

### Gaps
- I found no verified conversion or upsell rates for .org freemium themes, and no per-kit Elements earnings data for 2025–2026.

## Q6. Shopify Theme Store: requirements, fees, acceptance, pricing, sales

### Takeaway
Submission is free and Shopify takes 15% of gross from the first dollar (since Jan 2025), plus a 2.9% processing fee. The bar is very high. Since May 2025 themes must be original, not built on or derived from Dawn or Horizon. They need a /listings folder with presets, minimum Lighthouse performance (about 60) and accessibility (about 90) scores, and a full OS 2.0 feature set. Only about 245 paid third-party themes exist, which signals a small, curated store. Each listing can earn well (reported about $10k–$20k+ per theme per year, unverified), but getting accepted is hard and slow.

### Cited Findings
- No submission fee. Total theme revenues carry a 15% revenue share on gross, plus a 2.9% processing fee and sales tax — [Shopify dev: revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store/revenue-share)
- The 15% share began Jan 1, 2025, replacing the previous 0% subsidy under $1M. Themes have no exemption threshold — [lucentinnovation 2026](https://www.lucentinnovation.com/resources/it-insights/a-complete-guide-on-shopify-partners); [templatetrip 2026](https://www.templatetrip.com/blog/how-much-can-you-earn-selling-shopify-themes-2026)
- Themes must meet every requirement or be rejected — [Shopify Theme Store docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store)
- New submissions built on or derived from Dawn or Horizon are not eligible — [Shopify Theme Store requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store/requirements)
- **May 15, 2025 changes:** a /listings folder is required in the zip, and naming rules apply per preset. Existing themes had to update by June 22 and July 1, 2025 — [Shopify changelog May 15, 2025](https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/updated-shopify-theme-store-requirements-and-submission-process-effective-may-15-2025); [Updates guide](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store/success/updates)
- Lighthouse performance must be at least 60 and accessibility at least 90, averaged across key pages on desktop and mobile. This comes from a single blog, so verify it on Shopify's requirements page — [uncovercommerce](https://uncovercommerce.com/articles/what-it-takes-to-get-a-theme-approved-on-the-shopify-theme-store)
- Only one theme can be under review at a time for partners without a published theme. A Nov 2026 forum thread shows this rule being enforced — [Shopify submit theme docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store/review-process/submit-theme); [community thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/one-submission-in-progress-block-but-seven-themes-were-submitted-concurrently-how-is-in-progress-defined/711342)
- **Review time:** the official target is reportedly 5–10 business days, but developers report 7–14 days. Forum anecdotes describe reviews stuck "for over a month" — [uncovercommerce](https://uncovercommerce.com/articles/what-it-takes-to-get-a-theme-approved-on-the-shopify-theme-store); [community.shopify.dev](https://community.shopify.dev/t/my-theme-has-been-in-submitted-status-for-over-one-month/26746)
- **Store size (mid-2025):** 268 themes, of which 23 are free Shopify themes and 245 are paid third-party themes — [shopthemedetector](https://shopthemedetector.com/blog/shopify-theme-statistics/)
- **Pricing and earnings (secondary, unverified):** paid themes cost about $100–$400 as a one-time purchase. Per-theme earnings are reported as about $11k a year on average, or $10k–$20k+ a year — [uptek](https://uptek.com/shopify-statistics/themes/); [templatetrip 2026](https://www.templatetrip.com/blog/how-much-can-you-earn-selling-shopify-themes-2026)
- **Horizon competition:** Shopify's free Horizon family (10 themes, from 2025) competes directly with paid themes. Horizon 4.5.0 shipped Nov 10, 2026 — [fuelthemes](https://fuelthemes.net/shopify-horizon-theme-review/); [shopinfo](https://shopinfo.app/themes/horizon)

### Inferences
- The low theme count combined with strict originality, performance and accessibility rules suggests a low acceptance rate. A first-time solo author should expect months of work per theme, since a full OS 2.0 commerce theme (product, collection, cart, search, blog, multiple presets) is far larger than a one-page HTML template.
- **Rough net (inference):** a $350 theme keeps about $350 × (1 − 0.15 − 0.029) ≈ $287 per sale.

### Gaps
- I found no official acceptance or rejection rate and no count of new themes approved per year.
- I could not fetch the requirements page itself, so the exact Lighthouse thresholds are unverified.

## Q7. Shopify fit for one-page small-business templates; unofficial channels

### Takeaway
The fit is poor. Shopify themes must be complete e-commerce themes, and the seller's templates target service businesses (salon, clinic, gym, B&B, wedding, SaaS, portfolio) that rarely choose Shopify. The only fits are restaurant/café with merch or ordering, and perhaps the gym or salon templates for selling products alongside a booking app. Unofficial sales (ThemeForest Shopify category, Creative Market) avoid the Theme Store but now pay 50% on Envato and give lower visibility.

### Cited Findings
- Service businesses on Shopify usually run a general theme plus a booking app ("Shopify handles the calendar, the payment, the confirmation, and the reminder") — [fuelthemes: Shopify for service business](https://fuelthemes.net/shopify-for-service-business/)
- Niche third-party restaurant/booking Shopify themes exist outside the Theme Store (e.g. "Ap Rostay", Creative Market's "Restaurant Shopify Theme") — [Apollo Theme](https://apollotheme.leotheme.com/?p=62051); [Creative Market](https://creativemarket.com/LogicGoInfotech/291362368-Restaurant-Shopify-Theme)
- Service-oriented roundups recommend general themes such as Dawn for service businesses — [tiny-img](https://tiny-img.com/best-shopify-themes/for-selling-services/)
- The flat 50% Envato rate also applies to ThemeForest's Shopify category — [WP Gazette](https://wpgazette.com/industry-business/envato-50-revenue-share-exclusivity-ends/)

### Inferences
- Converting one-page service templates into Shopify themes means building commerce templates the original designs lack. This is effectively a new product, not a conversion.
- A lighter option, if any, is to sell **Shopify OS 2.0 sections/landing-page section packs** or page-builder (GemPages/PageFly) templates. These fit "one-page" better, but I did not research their market size.

### Gaps
- I found no market data on demand for Shopify themes for service businesses, and no sales data for ThemeForest Shopify one-page themes.

## Q8. Taiwan individual eligibility and payouts (Envato, Shopify)

### Takeaway
Envato pays individuals worldwide by PayPal (USD) or bank transfer, and Payoneer or Wise accounts can receive the bank transfer, so Taiwan should work. Shopify Partner payouts go by PayPal or bank, depending on country. I could not confirm Taiwan specifically for Shopify, but PayPal is usable in Taiwan through E.SUN Bank withdrawals. US tax forms (W-8BEN) apply, and the withholding rate for Taiwan was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Envato payout options are Bank Transfer or PayPal. Payoneer, Wise or Revolut count as Bank Transfer. One method covers both Market and Elements. The minimum is $50 a month — [Envato Payout System help](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/20535795834393-Getting-Started-with-the-Envato-Payout-System); [Introduction to Earnings](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000472943-Introduction-to-Earnings)
- Envato may withhold tax. Individuals complete a W-8BEN in Author Home — [Alto Accounting (UK-focused)](https://www.alto-accounting.com/insights/envato-creative-market-seller-tax-uk); [Becoming an Envato Author FAQs](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041052771-Becoming-an-Envato-Author-FAQs)
- Shopify Partner payouts go by PayPal or bank account, with options depending on country or region — [Shopify Help: manage payouts](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/manage-account/manage-payouts-invoices)
- PayPal funds in Taiwan can be withdrawn to E.SUN Bank (Global Pass), and sources conflict on whether other banks are supported — [orngstudio (TW)](https://orngstudio.com/en/blogs/tips/shopify-international-payment-taiwan); [hulkapps](https://www.hulkapps.com/blogs/shopify-payment-providers/paypal-shopify-integration-in-taiwan)

### Inferences
- Payouts are likely feasible on both platforms through PayPal → E.SUN. The seller probably already uses a similar route with Gumroad.
- Theme sales are not US-source royalties in every case, so the withholding treatment needs a Taiwan tax adviser.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm Taiwan in Shopify's Partner payout country list.
- I found no confirmed US withholding rate for Taiwan-resident individuals on Envato or Shopify earnings. Taiwan's tax treatment of foreign platform income was not researched.

## Q9. Competition, year-1 revenue, skills, and the 3–5 template test

### Takeaway
- **ThemeForest WordPress:** NO-GO as the main channel. The marketplace is in decline with a 50% cut, hard rejections are likely for generic one-pagers, and support burden is high. Year-1 revenue for a new author with a few themes is plausibly low four figures. An optional, small experiment is listing 1–2 themes non-exclusively, since exclusivity no longer exists.
- **WordPress block themes or Elementor kits on the seller's own Gumroad:** a sensible test. Convert 3–5 bestsellers, at a low upfront cost.
- **Shopify Theme Store:** NO-GO for these templates. They are not commerce themes, the originality and performance bar is high, review is slow, and Horizon offers strong free competition. It would only make sense as a separate, months-long product effort.

### Cited Findings
- Old anecdotes describe wide variance in new-author results, with slow starts common — [Medium](https://medium.com/@oleg_67082/thorny-way-to-sales-at-themeforest-net-ee5d370f4278); [Bluthemes first year (old)](http://www.bluthemes.com/blog/4/the-first-year-as-a-themeforest-author)
- Commentators push dual-channel or direct-sales strategies after the Envato change — [PureThemes](https://purethemes.net/envato-author-exclusivity-merchant-of-record/); [dmsi.gr](https://dmsi.gr/selling-wordpress-plugins-direct/); [wpbiz.dev](https://wpbiz.dev/growth/envato-commission/) (all have a stake in direct sales, flag bias)
- Shopify per-theme earnings are reported at about $10k–$20k a year (secondary, unverified, likely skewed toward successful listings) — [templatetrip](https://www.templatetrip.com/blog/how-much-can-you-earn-selling-shopify-themes-2026)

### Inferences
- **Skills needed:** WP needs theme.json, block patterns, templates, PHP basics, i18n (.pot files), WP coding standards and Theme Check, or alternatively Elementor. Shopify needs Liquid, OS 2.0 JSON templates, sections and blocks, a full commerce flow, Lighthouse and accessibility tuning, and preset listings.
- **Test design:** pick the 3–5 templates that sell best on Gumroad (restaurant, salon and similar). Build them as WP block themes with trilingual strings. Offer them as an add-on (for example an HTML + WP bundle tier) and measure attach rate over 60–90 days before deciding on more conversions or a ThemeForest listing.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025–2026 primary data on new-author first-year earnings on either platform. All revenue figures here are modelled or anecdotal.
