# Spanish (Spain/LatAm/US Hispanic) and Brazilian Portuguese markets for HTML one-page templates and website-building services (Taiwan-based solo seller)

Research note scope: 17 tool calls; research date 2026-11-22. In this session, direct page fetches of help.hotmart.com and gumroad.com were blocked by network settings. Those hosts must be allowed before the official pages can be checked. Hotmart and Gumroad facts below come from search-result snippets of their official help pages and from third-party blogs, so they are marked as such. Most cited sources are from 2025–2026; older ones are flagged.

## 1. Demand evidence: how SMBs get online (WhatsApp/Instagram vs standalone sites)

### Takeaway
In Mexico and Brazil, small businesses run mainly on social media and WhatsApp. Only a minority (about 10–17%) use their own website as a main channel. The demand for a standalone site exists, but it is a minority need, and it usually comes as "I need a site that sends people to my WhatsApp," not "I want an HTML template."

### Cited Findings
- **Mexico (GoDaddy Global Entrepreneurship Survey 2025):** the survey was run by Advanis in January 2025 with 3,504 small-business owners in 11 countries. In Mexico, 54% of small businesses operate mainly online. 41% do so mainly through social media, compared with 12% whose main channel is their own website. 51% cannot turn social activity into sales, and 54% say they cannot reach the right audience — [GoDaddy LatAm](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/latam/emprender/redes-sociales-pymes-mexico-2025); [Emprendedor.com](https://emprendedor.com/mas-del-50-de-las-pymes-mexicanas-no-logran-vender-en-redes-sociales/)
- **Mexico (GS1 México, 7th edition, reported July 2026):** 60% of SMBs sell through e-commerce, marketplaces or social media. The channels used for selling are Facebook 75%, Instagram 61% and WhatsApp 57% — [The Logistics World](https://thelogisticsworld.com/logistica-comercio-electronico/para-las-pymes-el-comercio-digital-ya-no-es-un-diferenciador-sino-un-requisito-basico-en-su-estrategia-de-negocio). The fieldwork year is unclear.
- **Mexico "Nenis" and solo entrepreneurs (Expansión, Dec 2025):** 72% of Nenis and 75% of solo entrepreneurs use social and WhatsApp channels as their main promotion route. Nenis invest about 20% of their income in social ads — [Expansión](https://expansion.mx/tecnologia/2025/12/05/las-nenis-invierten-20-de-sus-ingresos-en-publicidad-de-redes-sociales)
- HubSpot's claim that "92% of Mexican MiPyMEs use WhatsApp commercially" has no primary source and is unverified — [HubSpot ES blog](https://blog.hubspot.es/marketing/estrategia-marketing-pymes)
- **Brazil (Sebrae Pulso dos Pequenos Negócios, 12th edition, 2026 data):** 82% of MEIs and micro/small businesses use WhatsApp as their main channel for communication and sales. Businesses with their own online store fell from 14% in 2021 to 10% in 2026 — [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/whatsapp-se-consolida-nas-vendas-on-line-enquanto-facebook-e-lojas-proprias-perdem-folego/); [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/negocios/whatsapp-pequenos-negocios-sebrae/)
- **Brazil (Sebrae):** seven in ten small businesses have social profiles, and Instagram is the preferred platform. One write-up gives 64% on Instagram and 10% with their own website, but the year is unclear — [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/sete-em-cada-10-pequenos-negocios-tem-perfis-nas-redes-sociais-instagram-e-a-plataforma-preferida)
- **Brazil (Sebrae Transformação Digital 2022, older):** WhatsApp is the main digital sales tool for 74% of businesses, followed by social networks at 42% and their own website at 17% — [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/pequenos-negocios-ampliam-presenca-digital-para-conquistar-clientes). Sebrae also published a 2025 Maturidade Digital study — [DataSebrae PDF](https://datasebrae.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Apresentacao-Maturidade-Digital-2025-v1-Porte-setor-e-atividades.pdf); its website percentages were not extracted.
- **Brazil (GoDaddy 2025 survey):** nearly half of Brazilian small businesses operate mainly online, through websites, marketplaces or social media — [GoDaddy BR](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/br/artigos/empreendedores-redes-sociais)
- **Brazil:** SMB online sales grew more than 1,200% from 2019 to 2024, according to government and Central Bank figures, with Pix credited as a driver — [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/brazils-small-businesses-drive-e-commerce-boom-as-digital-payments-reshape-retail/). The US Commercial Service expects Brazilian e-commerce to reach US$36.3B in 2025 and expects Pix to overtake cards as the top online payment method in 2025 — [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/knowledge-product/brazil-ecommerce)
- **Spanish-language template supply on Etsy:** existing listings are mostly Canva-based website templates delivered as a PDF with a Canva link. They target UGC creators, social media managers and coaches, are often sold with MRR/PLR resale rights, and are usually non-refundable. Prices in the snapshot ran from about €11.91 to €37.11 — [Etsy ES shop strimlab](https://www.etsy.com/es/shop/strimlab); [Etsy MX AprilWebDesigns](https://www.etsy.com/mx/shop/AprilWebDesigns); [Etsy AsistenteVirtualNely](https://www.etsy.com/shop/AsistenteVirtualNely). Hotmart also hosts similar Spanish products, for example "Portafolio UGC, Sitio Web" — [Hotmart marketplace](https://hotmart.com/es/marketplace/productos/portafolio-ugc-sitio-web/E97742214N)

### Inferences
- The buyer for raw HTML files in these markets is mostly a freelancer, agency or tech-savvy owner, not the end SMB. End SMBs mostly live on Instagram and WhatsApp, and when they want a site, they want a done-for-you service or a no-code (Canva/Wix-style) template.
- The positioning that fits the data is "a professional one-page site that turns Instagram followers into WhatsApp chats and bookings": WhatsApp click-to-chat as the main CTA, plus an Instagram feed or link, Google Maps and opening hours. A plain "HTML template" pitch fits poorly.
- Spanish-language Etsy demand seems tilted toward Canva templates for personal brands. HTML/code templates are a smaller, more technical niche there.

### Gaps
- No search-volume data (Google Trends or Keyword Planner) was retrieved for "plantilla web", "plantilla de página web", "template de site" or "landing page" by country. Run Google Trends or Keyword Planner directly.
- No reliable 2025–2026 website-ownership figures were found for Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Spain or US Hispanic businesses.
- No data was found on Linktree-style tools in LatAm (Linktree, Beacons, or WhatsApp catalog as a substitute for a site).

## 2. Platforms: fees, payout to Taiwan, registration

### Takeaway
Gumroad, which the seller already uses, remains the lowest-friction option: it is merchant of record, has built-in PPP pricing and already pays out to Taiwan. Hotmart is the only big LatAm/Brazil platform with a credible route for producers outside Brazil, but Taiwan eligibility is unconfirmed. Kiwify and, likely, Eduzz assume a Brazilian CPF or CNPJ, which in practice rules them out. Workana, 99Freelas and Fiverr are channels for the customization service, and their commissions are steep.

### Cited Findings
- **Gumroad fees:** 10% + $0.50 per direct sale, and 30% on Discover marketplace sales. Sources conflict on whether card or PayPal processing is extra — [checkoutpage.com](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/gumroad-is-going-to-increase-its-fees-to-10-for-everyone-jan-31st-ff59a0963e); [Sellfy 2026](https://sellfy.com/blog/gumroad-pricing/)
- **Gumroad as merchant of record:** Gumroad's help center says it acts as merchant of record for all sales to EU and UK buyers and collects and remits VAT. Third-party sources say this has applied broadly since January 2025. Competitor-written sources dispute it, but they look outdated — [Gumroad Help: VAT](https://gumroad.com/help/article/10-dealing-with-vat) (snippet only; fetch blocked); [Paddle comparison](https://paddle.com/compare/gumroad) (competitor)
- **Gumroad PPP:** a toggle in Settings. Discounts range from 20% to 60%, are based on World Bank data and refresh weekly. Sellers can cap the maximum discount, and the discount applies automatically with no code needed — [Gumroad Help: PPP](https://gumroad.com/help/article/327-purchasing-power-parity); [Gumroad blog](https://gumroad.com/blog/p/introducing-purchasing-power-parity). Eligible one-time card purchases can be charged in the buyer's local currency — [Gumroad Help: currency](https://gumroad.com/help/article/46-what-currency-does-gumroad-use)
- **Hotmart fees:** 9.9% plus a fixed fee per sale. In Latin America this is reported as 9.9% + US$1 — [Hotmart ES blog](https://hotmart.com/es/blog/ventajas-de-hotmart). In Brazil the fixed fee was R$1. The official help center says that from 21 Sep 2026 the player fee is folded into the fixed transaction fee, and third parties put the new fixed fee at R$2.49 — [Hotmart Help: taxas](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/208298448/quais-sao-as-taxas-cobradas-pela-hotmart-); [Tactus](https://tactus.com.br/taxas-da-hotmart-para-produtor/). One source says there is no extra fee for international sales — [viniensina](https://viniensina.com.br/?p=4267). Tax on the fee depends on the account's country and the product type — [Hotmart Help](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-BR/article/quais-sao-as-tarifas-de-servico-cobradas-pela-hotmart-/208298448)
- **Hotmart payouts for producers outside Brazil:**
  - Payoneer is available only for USD commissions, not EUR, MXN or GBP.
  - The automatic monthly transfer has no withdrawal fee. Manual withdrawals have a minimum of US$20 plus a US$1.99 fee (or €20 + €1.99). Payoneer withdrawals have a minimum of US$50 and a maximum of US$5,000. Payoneer applies its own FX rate.
  - Wise accounts are not accepted, whatever the country.
  - The help center has an article on two-day payout for eligible producers registered in "other countries outside Brazil".
  - Sources: [Hotmart Help: saque](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/216440207/como-sacar-a-minha-comissao-); [Hotmart Help: transferência automática](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/22224013803149); [Hotmart Help: 2 dias fora do Brasil](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/36866116752525/como-funciona-o-prazo-de-recebimento-em-2-dias-para-produtores-elegiveis-cadastrados-nos-demais-paises-fora-do-brasil-); [Hotmart payment policy](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/legal/politicas-de-pagamento) (snippets only)
- **Hotmart account country and reach:** the account country is set from the device's IP address, and changing it requires support validation. Producers living in other countries have more limited payment methods. Hotmart sells in 188+ countries, which refers to buyers — [Hotmart Help: alterar país](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/360018816832/como-alterar-o-pais-cadastrado-na-minha-conta-da-hotmart-); [Hotmart international sales blog](https://hotmart.com/en/blog/how-international-sales-work-at-hotmart)
- **Kiwify:** third-party sources say registration requires a Brazilian CPF or CNPJ and a bank account in the same name. Fee figures conflict: 8.99% + R$2.49, 7.9% + R$1 (with a R$4.73 withdrawal fee), or 4.99%. Kiwify's terms name a Brazilian operating entity, Kiwify Pagamentos — [Remessa Online](https://www.remessaonline.com.br/blog/kiwify/); [Herospark](https://herospark.com/blog/quanto-a-kiwify-cobra-por-venda/amp/); [Tactus CNPJ](https://tactus.com.br/cnpj-kiwify/); [Kiwify terms](https://kiwify.com/terms-of-use)
- **Eduzz:** not researched directly. It appears only in comparison posts — [freelasemcrise comparison](https://www.freelasemcrise.com.br/blog/hotmart-kiwify-eduzz-comparativo)
- **Workana:**
  - Commission: one source says it is set per contract at 0–15%, and the client pays an extra 5.5%. An older tiered scale was 20% up to US$300, 10% from US$301 to 3,000, and 5% above US$3,000. The help center says the fee can fall to 5% in long-term client relationships.
  - Payoneer withdrawals: minimum US$50, up to three business days. Funds are held for 14 days.
  - Sources: [Workana Help: cost](https://help.workana.com/hc/es/articles/360040846094--Cu%C3%A1nto-cuesta-usar-Workana); [Workana Help: Payoneer](https://help.workana.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041873313-Withdraws-with-Payoneer); [Global66 (Aug 2026)](https://www.global66.com/blog/mejores-plataformas-freelance-chile/); [ARQ (older)](https://www.arqfinance.com/es-MX/blog/freelancer-tips/freelancer-vs-workana). Sources conflict.
- **Payoneer:** USD-to-USD withdrawal to a local account costs 0.5%. Withdrawals in other currencies cost 1–4% — [Payoneer fees](https://www.payoneer.com/es/about/fees/)
- **99Freelas:**
  - Payment-method fees (reported, from documentation updated Sept 2026): Pix 2%, card 4.99% + R$0.50, boleto 1.79% + R$0.69, PayPal 6.40% + R$0.60.
  - Paid plans cost R$54.90–89.90 per month.
  - The market is Brazil-oriented, and no foreign-freelancer onboarding info was found.
  - Sources: [ecarts](https://ecarts.com.br/tecnologia/99freelas-em-2026-quanto-paga-taxas-e-como-ganhar-dinheiro/); [gameoverdrive](https://gameoverdrive.com.br/99freelas-como-funciona/)
- **Fiverr:** has Portuguese-language help and withdrawal pages. A Brazilian guide recommends Wise over PayPal for payouts. Portuguese-language website gigs exist on br.fiverr.com — [Fiverr Help PT](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/pt/articles/360010530058-Sacar-seus-ganhos-e-gerenciar-m%C3%A9todos-de-saque); [freelasemcrise Fiverr 2026](https://www.freelasemcrise.com.br/blog/fiverr-para-brasileiro-2026); [Fiverr BR gig example](https://br.fiverr.com/hannahsmith992/create-and-setup-a-new-website-for-your-small-business)

### Inferences
- **Recommended stack:**
  1. Keep Gumroad as the store, with ES and PT product pages and PPP turned on.
  2. Test Hotmart only if a Taiwan-registered account can withdraw in USD to Payoneer. Hotmart's value is its affiliate network and local payment methods (Pix, boleto, OXXO).
  3. Skip Kiwify and Eduzz.
  4. Use Fiverr and Workana gigs in ES/PT for the customization service.
- Hotmart's 9.9% + US$1 fee is similar to Gumroad's 10% + $0.50. The decision should rest on local payment methods and affiliates, not on fees.

### Gaps
- Whether Hotmart accepts Taiwan as a producer country was not confirmed. Check the Hotmart help center (blocked here) or ask support.
- Etsy specifics were not researched: Taiwan seller eligibility, the US$0.20 listing fee, the 6.5% transaction fee, and Etsy Payments payout to Taiwan. Etsy's own seller docs should be checked.
- Freelancer.com fees were not researched.
- Whether Gumroad's PPP checks card country or IP was not confirmed.

## 3. Purchasing power and typical prices

### Takeaway
Done-for-you one-page sites cost roughly US$100–600 in Brazil and US$350–800 in Mexico. The seller's US$249–449 customization price is mid-range locally and cannot compete with local freelancers on price. US$39 per template is high against the €12–37 Spanish Etsy Canva templates and US$12–17 ThemeForest HTML landing pages. PPP pricing, or a lower regional price, is advisable for LatAm.

- **Service:** US$249 sits within the local mid-range. With weak trust signals (a foreign seller, a time zone 11–14 hours away, possibly no local language on calls), it needs to sit at or below local freelancer prices. US Hispanic and Spain buyers can likely bear the current US$249–449.
- **Templates:** PPP at 40–60% would bring a US$39 template to about US$16–23 in Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Brazil, which matches market prices. A US$199 bundle would land around US$80–120.

## 4. Competition

### Takeaway
The HTML landing-template market is already saturated and cheap globally. Few templates come Spanish- or Portuguese-native, but buyers there mostly choose Canva or no-code templates. The gap is narrow: localized, WhatsApp-first HTML templates for local SMB niches.

### Cited Findings
- ThemeForest has a "portuguese" search and some Spanish-supporting landing templates, and most landing items have modest sales in the tens — [ThemeForest: portuguese](https://themeforest.net/search/portuguese); [ThemeForest landing search](https://themeforest.net/search/html%20landing%20page?page=25)
- TemplateMonster runs a Portuguese storefront for HTML/JS landing templates — [TemplateMonster PT-BR](https://www.templatemonster.com/pt-br/html-javascript-landing-page-templates/?rating=2)
- Spanish-language Etsy competition is Canva website templates for personal brands, often bundled with MRR/PLR resale rights — see section 1 Etsy sources.

### Inferences
- Differentiators worth stressing: native ES and PT copy (not machine translation), niche-specific local content (taquería, barbería, clínica dental, pousada), WhatsApp, Pix and Mercado Pago integration, and a language switcher, which is useful in Spain, the US Hispanic market, tourism B&Bs, and bilingual clinics.

### Gaps

- No sales data was found for top Spanish or Portuguese sellers.

## 5. Legal and compliance

### Takeaway
Selling through a merchant of record such as Gumroad, Hotmart or Etsy largely removes VAT/IVA registration duties. Selling directly, for example via a Stripe/PayPal checkout on the seller's own site, creates obligations: Mexico requires foreign digital providers to register with the SAT and charge 16% IVA, and the EU requires OSS VAT. Privacy rules (GDPR for Spain, LGPD for Brazil) apply mainly to customer data the seller collects, such as service clients and mailing lists, and to forms built into the templates.

### Cited Findings
- **Gumroad:** acts as merchant of record for EU and UK sales and collects and remits VAT — [Gumroad Help](https://gumroad.com/help/article/10-dealing-with-vat) (snippet)
- **Mexico (IVA on foreign digital services):** since June 2020, foreign digital service providers without a Mexican establishment must register in the RFC with the SAT and charge 16% IVA (Ley del IVA arts. 18-H Bis to 18-H Quintus). Intermediary platforms withhold IVA from unregistered foreign providers. Service blocking toward Mexico is the last-resort sanction. The 2026 economic package changed rules for digital platforms — [SAT disposiciones](https://www.sat.gob.mx/minisitio/PlataformasTecnologicas/PlataformasTecnologicas_ServiciosDigitales/documentos/DisposicionesFiscales_sobre_plataformastecnologicas.pdf); [SAT intermediación](https://www.sat.gob.mx/minisitio/PlataformasTecnologicas/PlataformasTecnologicas_Intermediacion/documentos/DisposicionesFiscales_intermediacion.pdf); [Contadores México](https://www.contadoresmexico.org.mx/Boletin/IVA-plataformas-digitales-operaciones-residentes-extranjero); [EY Paquete Económico 2026](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/es-mx/technical/tax/documents/ey-tax-flas-paquete-economico-2026-plataformas-digitales.pdf); [Kintsugi Mexico VAT 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/es-419/sales-tax-guides/latam/mexico). Secondary sources disagree on whether platform withholding is full or partial.
- **Hotmart tax on fees:** depends on the account's country and the product type — [Hotmart Help](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-BR/article/quais-sao-as-tarifas-de-servico-cobradas-pela-hotmart-/208298448)

### Inferences
- **Service work** (US$249–449 customization) is invoiced directly or through Fiverr/Workana, and is not covered by Gumroad's merchant-of-record role. For Mexican clients, a foreign provider of a custom service (not an automated digital service) may fall outside the 18-H digital-services regime. Not verified; needs an accountant.
- **Templates with contact forms or analytics:** buyers in Spain and Brazil will need cookie consent and a privacy-policy page. Shipping a GDPR/LGPD-ready privacy-notice placeholder and an optional cookie banner is a selling point.

### Gaps
- No source was retrieved on whether Gumroad collects IVA in Mexico, Chile or Colombia, or ICMS/ISS in Brazil, or whether its merchant-of-record coverage is worldwide.
- No LGPD source was retrieved. Its obligations for foreign processors of Brazilian data and the ANPD's international-transfer rules should be checked.
- No source was retrieved on Spain or EU consumer rules for digital content (the 14-day withdrawal right is waived only with express consent at checkout).
- No source was retrieved on Chile, Colombia or Argentina VAT on foreign digital services. These countries are known to have such regimes, but this is unverified here.

## 6. Localization beyond translation

### Takeaway
Localization for these markets is mostly functional. WhatsApp click-to-chat should be the primary CTA, and checkout depends on local payment methods (Pix in Brazil, Mercado Pago links in LatAm, Bizum in Spain), along with local formats.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp is the main sales and communication channel for 82% of Brazilian small businesses (2026). In Mexico, WhatsApp is used by 57% of SMBs that sell through social and marketplace channels — [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/whatsapp-se-consolida-nas-vendas-on-line-enquanto-facebook-e-lojas-proprias-perdem-folego/); [The Logistics World](https://thelogisticsworld.com/logistica-comercio-electronico/para-las-pymes-el-comercio-digital-ya-no-es-un-diferenciador-sino-un-requisito-basico-en-su-estrategia-de-negocio)
- Pix is expected to become Brazil's top online payment method in 2025 — [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/knowledge-product/brazil-ecommerce)
- Hotmart offers local payment methods per country and lets sellers enable them per product — [Hotmart Help: métodos de pagamento](https://help.hotmart.com/pt-br/article/25588460435085/quais-sao-as-metodos-de-pagamento-disponiveis-e-como-habilita-los-para-o-meu-produto-)

### Inferences (checklist; not individually sourced)
- **WhatsApp:**
  - A floating `wa.me/<number>?text=` button with a pre-filled message in ES or PT.
  - The number format must include the country code (+52 Mexico, +55 Brazil, +57 Colombia, +54 9 Argentina mobiles, +56 Chile, +34 Spain).
- **Payments:**
  - Brazil: a Pix copy-paste or QR block.
  - LatAm: a Mercado Pago payment-link button.
  - Spain: a Bizum mention.
- **Formats:**
  - Brazil: R$ 1.234,56.
  - Mexico: $1,234.56 MXN.
  - Spain: 1.234,56 €.
  - Dates are DD/MM/YYYY.
  - Address formats: CEP (Brazil), C.P. (Mexico and Spain), colonia (Mexico), bairro (Brazil).
- **Language variants:**
  - Neutral Latin American Spanish versus Spain Spanish: "ustedes" vs "vosotros", and vocabulary such as celular/móvil or turno/cita.
  - Brazilian Portuguese, not European Portuguese.

## 8. Verdict inputs: revenue potential vs effort

### Takeaway
The template-only market in ES/PT looks small and low-priced. The better bet is a low-cost add-on (ES and PT strings on Gumroad with PPP) plus a WhatsApp-first, done-for-you service aimed at US Hispanic and Spain clients, who pay more. LatAm and Brazil would be a volume or affiliate play through Hotmart, if Taiwan registration works.

### Cited Findings
- Only 10–17% of Brazilian and Mexican small businesses use their own website as a main channel — [GoDaddy MX 2025](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/latam/emprender/redes-sociales-pymes-mexico-2025); [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/whatsapp-se-consolida-nas-vendas-on-line-enquanto-facebook-e-lojas-proprias-perdem-folego/)

- Local freelancers build one-pagers for about R$500–3,000 in Brazil and MXN 7,000–14,000 in Mexico — see section 3.

### Inferences
- The upside is plausible but modest. If ES and PT listings added 10–30 template sales a month at a PPP-blended US$20–30, that would be about US$200–900 a month gross, before Gumroad's roughly 10% fee. Speculative: there is no demand data to calibrate it.
- **Payback:** a one-off cost of about US$1–3k plus 50–100 hours would be recovered in roughly 2–12 months under that assumption.
- **Service:** US Hispanic and Spain clients can pay US$249–449. LatAm and Brazil clients will push toward US$100–250, competing with local freelancers who speak the language and share the time zone. Expect low conversion without local-language sales calls or WhatsApp support.
- **Lowest-risk sequence:**
  1. Ship neutral Spanish only for the top 5–8 niches (restaurant, salon or barbershop, clinic or dentist, gym, B&B or pousada) on Gumroad with PPP on. Measure for 60–90 days.
  2. Then add PT-BR and a Hotmart listing.
  3. Run ES and PT Fiverr gigs for customization.
