# Converting HTML templates to Framer / Webflow / Notion / Canva: go/no-go evidence (researched 2026-11-22)

Method note: in this session WebFetch could not reach framer.com or webflow.com (the hosts are not on the session's network allowlist), so every finding below comes from search-result snippets. Many sources are vendor blogs or competitor storefronts (Getly, Contra, CartMango, Checkout Page) with a commercial interest. Each one is flagged where it matters. Check official pages before acting on fees or payouts.

## 1. Framer Marketplace

### Takeaway
Framer is the most active template market. It takes a 0% cut on paid templates, and since Framer 3.0 (June 16, 2026) a listing no longer needs approval before it goes live. That same change made the market crowded: there are about 10k templates and more than 7,000 creators, and top creators say sales fell sharply after it. Payouts to an individual in Taiwan are not confirmed and are the main blocker, because Framer's payout tooling is built on Stripe and Stripe does not onboard Taiwan businesses. Verdict: a conditional GO for a small test, only after payout is confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Enrollment:** publishing a Template, Plugin, Component or Vector Set in the Marketplace automatically enrolls you in the Creator Program. — [Framer Help: How the Creator Program works](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-the-creator-program-works/)
- **Fees:** Framer says it takes a 0% cut and that creators keep 100% of paid-template revenue. — [Framer creators page](https://framer.com/creators); [Framer Help](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-the-creator-program-works/). A 2026 third-party blog instead says Framer takes "a smaller platform fee" but gives no figure. — [framerwebsites.com](https://framerwebsites.com/blog/framer-creator-program-guide). Trust the official page.
- **Second income stream:** creators also earn affiliate/partner commissions through Dub when buyers sign up for paid Framer plans. Per a third-party guide, commissions sit pending for 30 days, the minimum payout is $200, and payouts run monthly. — [framerhub.io](https://framerhub.io/blog/framer-affiliate-program-guide). Framer's own Dub help page says that **if Stripe isn't available in your country**, you may need a US LLC or a Wise-based "Stripe-compatible account", and that Framer does not withhold taxes. — [Framer Help: Getting started with Dub](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/getting-started-with-dub/)
- **Review:**
  - Before June 2026 every template was reviewed by hand. Common rejection reasons were weak mobile layouts, fixed widths and heights, inconsistent spacing and type, generic content, and designs too close to existing listings. Review took about 1–3 weeks, and many templates were approved on the 2nd or 3rd try. — [framerwebsites.com guide](https://framerwebsites.com/blog/how-to-sell-framer-templates); [Ahshan, ex-reviewer checklist, X, Dec 2024 (anecdotal)](https://x.com/itsmeahshan/status/1871557071994261985); [yev on X, 2024 (anecdotal)](https://x.com/yev_henii/status/1815313078071988583)
  - **Framer 3.0 (June 16, 2026) removed the pre-publish review gate.** The Marketplace now sits inside "Community", and Framer staff still curate featured items and ranking. — [Framer blog: Framer 3](https://www.framer.com/blog/framer-3/); [Framer blog: Marketplace becoming part of Community](https://www.framer.com/blog/marketplace-new-framer-community/); [Humblytics summary](https://humblytics.com/blog/framer-3-agents-branching-community)
  - Framer's stated reason: the old review system was slow, created bottlenecks, "was not always fair", and made it hard for newcomers to get in. — [Framer blog](https://www.framer.com/blog/marketplace-new-framer-community/)
- **Size and competition:**
  - Framer says more than 7,000 creators sell on the Marketplace. — [Framer 3 coverage](https://www.framer.com/blog/framer-3/)
  - An independent tracker (Offset) counted 10,288 templates in a recent snapshot (third-party figure). — via [search summary of uxdesign.cc / allaboutframer](https://uxdesign.cc/framer-is-killing-the-marketplace-or-are-they-cc96a67763b3)
  - **Backlash after 3.0:** "Framer 3.0 killed the marketplace" (community post, anecdotal). — [Framer community](https://www.framer.com/community/posts/YPRZZsoLusd5S5wsZ1PXmB/). "Framer Changed Their Marketplace — And People Are Pissed". — [Medium](https://medium.com/@arronbydesign/the-marketplace-gave-everyone-access-and-people-are-pissed-3736963ca858)
  - Erfan on X spoke with 10–15 top creators and reports "sales have slowed dramatically… some have completely dried up" (anecdotal, 2026). — [X/@pentaclay](https://x.com/pentaclay/status/2080192027317121307)
  - One commentator notes that newcomers can now enter faster but discovery is harder. — [Basit Khan on X](https://x.com/basit_designs/status/2067377448824778844)
- **Earnings evidence:**
  - Framer reportedly paid creators $6.5M in 2025, and its creator page cites $753k paid in one strong month. — via [framer.com/creators](https://www.framer.com/creators) (search summary, not verified on the page)
  - Top creators report about $20–36k/month, all self-reported and pre-3.0:
    - Dmitrienko: $36,735 in one month ($28,487 direct template sales plus $8,248 partner/affiliate).
    - Hamza Ehsan: $11,630/month (Aug 2024) rising to $24,080/month in 2025; he also claims more than $300k lifetime from the creator/affiliate program alone.
    - A 2025 monthly series of about $21.7k–$24k is attributed to "Ramish", but sources conflict and it may be the same data as Hamza's.
    - Sources: [palai.media case studies](https://palai.media/en/articles/yak-zaroblyayut-na-framer-shablonah-4-kejsy); [allaboutframer.com 2026](https://allaboutframer.com/how-much-can-you-actually-earn-with-framer-in-2026-(real-numbers)); [stealwhatworks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/framer-templates-over-10k-month-now)
  - "Total commissions from one landing page template, 4 months, zero marketing" (community post; I could not read the amount). — [Framer community](https://www.framer.com/community/posts/EWDPgfjZ6Q9PB7FcUSuXp8/). A related headline: "This template earned him $10,000 in 4 months". — [goodreads blog repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25005406-this-template-earned-him-10-000-in-4-months)
  - Newcomers, per blogs (low reliability):
    - about $100–500/month, mostly from launch spikes — [webpo.space](https://webpo.space/blog/how-to-make-money-with-framer-templates)
    - $2–7k/month for "growing" creators with 5–10 templates plus active marketing — [allaboutframer](https://allaboutframer.com/how-much-can-you-actually-earn-with-framer-in-2026-(real-numbers))
    - revenue is "increasingly concentrated" in a small group of creators — [allaboutframer](https://allaboutframer.com/where-to-submit-your-framer-templates-(and-actually-get-sales))
- **Prices:**
  - Marketplace snapshots show many paid templates at $49, $79 and $99, plus many free ones. — [Framer Marketplace templates](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/); [Ecommerce category](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/categories/ecommerce/)
  - Guide bands: simple one-page landing pages $19–39; multi-page CMS sites $49–149. — [blog.framerwebsites.com](https://blog.framerwebsites.com/framer-marketplace-guide/)
  - Opinion pieces argue that $69–149 premium pricing beats $29. — [stealwhatworks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/framer-templates-over-10k-month-now)
- **Categories that sell:** agency, portfolio and professional-services templates are the strongest public examples. SaaS, AI, real estate and ecommerce are also popular. — [stealwhatworks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/framer-templates-over-10k-month-now); [PremiumPress 2026](https://www.premiumpress.com/blog/framer-templates/)
- **Localization:** this is a paid add-on for the site owner. The common figure is $20/locale/month on yearly billing ($25 monthly), but caps conflict between sources (up to 20 locales, or Basic 2 / Pro 10 / Scale 20). AI translation uses AI credits. — [superblog.ai](https://superblog.ai/blog/framer-pricing/); [brixtemplates](https://brixtemplates.com/blog/framer-limitations-you-should-know-before-building-your-site); [demanddesigner](https://demanddesigner.com/blog-index/framer-localization-cost)
- **Selling off-platform:** Framer templates are also sold through remix links on Gumroad, Contra and similar stores. — [Contra](https://contra.com/sell-framer-templates); [Getly](https://www.getly.store/sell/framer-templates) (both are competitor storefronts)

### Inferences
- **The trilingual switcher is a weak selling point in Framer.** A buyer would pay about $40/month extra for two extra locales to keep it ($20 × 2). The workaround is to ship one locale and keep the 中文 content in a CMS or separate pages, which removes much of the seller's differentiator. Marketing it as "localization-ready, 繁中/简中 copy included" is still a possible niche angle, since few Marketplace templates target Taiwan or HK SMBs.
- **Post-3.0 there is no approval hurdle, so difficulty has moved from "getting accepted" to "getting discovered".**
  - The pre-3.0 creator earnings ($20–36k/month) describe a market that no longer exists in that form.
  - A realistic year-1 for a new creator with 5–10 templates is low hundreds to low thousands of USD per month at best. Affiliate commissions are often a large share of that.
- **Which templates fit:**
  - Best fit, matching what sells: SaaS landing, portfolio, agency-like pages.
  - Restaurant, salon, gym, clinic and B&B fit less well, because Framer's buyers lean toward designers, startups and freelancers rather than local SMB owners. Still, local-business niches are less crowded.
  - The Motion editions are a good fit for Framer's animation strengths.
- **Rebuild effort:** HTML cannot be imported into Framer, so each template has to be rebuilt by hand with breakpoints, components, and text and color styles. Assume roughly 1–3 days per one-page template for a skilled designer. This is my estimate; no source gives an hour count.

### Gaps
- **Payout mechanics for Marketplace template sales (not affiliate) and whether a Taiwan individual can receive them:**
  - I found no official Framer page on this.
  - The Dub help page implies Stripe is required and suggests a US LLC or Wise workaround.
  - Stripe does not onboard Taiwan-based businesses (see section 6).
  - Must confirm with Framer support before building anything.
- The amount in the "one landing page template, 4 months" post could not be read.
- There is no official post-3.0 sales data for new creators.

## 2. Webflow Marketplace

### Takeaway
On paper Webflow's terms are the best: a 95% creator share, a "sell anywhere" fulfillment link, and 3–5 day review. But a creator who publishes dashboard snapshots reports Marketplace sales fell about 60% between March and August 2026, and Webflow barely promotes templates. Payout needs Stripe onboarding after the first approved template; Taiwan appears on Stripe's Global Payouts list, but Webflow doesn't confirm it and an old forum thread reports a Taiwan creator couldn't get paid. Rebuild effort is high because Webflow requires CMS skill and GSAP interactions. Verdict: NO-GO for now, or a low-priority later test.

### Cited Findings
- **Revenue share:** creators earn 95% of each Marketplace template sale; Webflow keeps 5% for payment processing. — [Webflow: Become a Template Designer](https://webflow.com/templates/applications); [Webflow updates: template creator enhancements](https://webflow.com/updates/template-creator-enhancements); [Webflow blog: "Earn more, sell anywhere"](https://webflow.com/blog/template-creator-enhancements)
  - Competitor storefronts claim Webflow takes about 50%. That looks outdated or misleading. — [Getly](https://www.getly.store/sell/webflow-templates); [CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-website-templates/)
- **Sell anywhere:** creators can generate a fulfillment link per template, list it on their own site or another marketplace, and set their own price. — [Webflow updates](https://webflow.com/updates/template-creator-enhancements); [Webflow forum announcement](https://discourse.webflow.com/t/sell-anywhere-earn-more-exciting-updates-to-template-creator-program/328589)
- **Requirements:**
  - Applicants need design skill plus deep Webflow CMS and/or Ecommerce practice, and must accept feedback from the Template QA team. — [Webflow applications](https://webflow.com/templates/applications)
  - Review typically takes 3–5 days. — [Webflow updates](https://webflow.com/updates/template-creator-enhancements)
  - GSAP-powered Webflow Interactions became the default for Marketplace templates from May 1, 2026; avoid legacy interactions. — [Webflow submission guidelines](https://webflow.com/templates/submission-guidelines)
- **Payouts:**
  - Stripe onboarding is offered only after a first paid template is approved. — [Webflow Stripe onboarding guide](https://webflow.com/marketplace/services/stripe-onboarding-guide)
  - Webflow warns that Stripe "does not support cross-border payouts for all countries" and that creators in unsupported countries must meet "special requirements". — [same guide](https://webflow.com/marketplace/services/stripe-onboarding-guide)
  - Stripe Global Payouts lists Taiwan as a recipient country (TWD, by wire). Required details: email, company name, address, phone. — [Stripe recipient requirements](https://docs.stripe.com/global-payouts/recipient-requirements); [Stripe recipient countries](https://docs.stripe.com/global-payouts/recipient-countries)
  - An older forum thread says a Taiwan creator could not use PayPal or Stripe (OLD, possibly outdated). — [Webflow forum](https://discourse.webflow.com/t/payment-for-sold-templates/216408)
- **Market direction in 2026:**
  - Creator Zoya Aqib reports Marketplace-wide template sales fell from about 11,500 (rolling 30 days, March 2026) to about 4,500 (August 2026), roughly −60%. She says AI does not explain the whole drop, the marketplace has more than 7,000 templates, and templates are barely promoted by Webflow. Single-creator data; she claims Webflow acknowledged the decline. — [zoyaqib.com Sept 2026 update](https://www.zoyaqib.com/post/september-2026-update-is-it-still-worth-selling-webflow-templates-in-2026); [zoyaqib.com 2026](https://www.zoyaqib.com/post/is-it-still-worth-designing-and-selling-webflow-templates-in-2026)
  - AI site builders now solve the entry-level problem templates used to serve. One AI tool charges $14 per generated site, versus Marketplace templates at $49–199. — [igorev.agency](https://www.igorev.agency/blog/webflow-templates-vs-ai-website-builders-why-the-market-changed)
  - A claimed "412% growth in AI-generated templates" figure could not be verified. — [pravinkumar.co](https://www.pravinkumar.co/blog/ai-generated-webflow-templates-build-studios-june-2026)
  - Earnings guides for 2026: [nikolaibain.com](https://www.nikolaibain.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-from-webflow-templates); [stealwhatworks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/webflow-templates-over-10k-month-now) (unverified)

### Inferences
- Webflow's buyers are agencies, startups and marketers paying Webflow hosting, a poor match for Taiwan restaurant or salon owners.
- Webflow localization is also a paid add-on, so the trilingual angle has the same cost problem as on Framer.
- Rebuilding needs CMS structure and GSAP work. Estimate about 3–5 days per template (my estimate).
- Given the reported −60% sales trend and the unconfirmed Taiwan payout, the expected return is weak.

### Gaps
- I could not open Webflow's official submission guidelines for current price floors or ceilings and page-count rules.
- There is no confirmation of which payout countries Webflow supports.
- There is no official Webflow statement on the 2026 sales decline.

## 3. Notion websites (Notion Sites, Super.so, Potion) and Notion Marketplace

### Takeaway
The Notion Marketplace pays out through Stripe and requires a waitlist and approval. Fees are 8% + $0.40, plus 1% FX for non-US creators. I found no evidence of demand for Notion-based small-business websites. Notion buyers want productivity templates, and Notion sites lack the design control a restaurant or salon page needs. Verdict: NO-GO.

### Cited Findings
- **Fees:** 8% + $0.40 per transaction according to Notion's help page. — [Notion Help: Sell templates on Notion Marketplace](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace)
  - Some 2026 guides say 10% + $0.40. — [zackliu substack](https://zackliu.substack.com/p/where-to-sell-notion-templates); [checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/how-to-sell-notion-templates)
- **Payouts:**
  - Non-US creators pay an extra 1% FX fee.
  - Payouts run biweekly on Thursdays with a $20 minimum, and some countries have higher minimums because of Stripe.
  - There is an eligible-country list on the page, but I could not see it.
  - Source: [Notion Help](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace)
- **Access:** sellers join a waitlist, wait for Notion team approval (a few weeks to months, per guides), then onboard with Stripe. — [Notion Help](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace); [eden.so 2026](https://eden.so/blog/how-to-sell-notion-templates/)

### Inferences
- Notion payouts run on Stripe, which likely excludes a Taiwan individual (see section 6).
- Super.so and Potion sites are built from a Notion page plus a theme, so a design-led HTML template cannot be translated faithfully.
- The buyers are creators and solopreneurs, not local SMBs.

### Gaps
- I found no sources on Super.so or Potion template marketplaces (whether third-party themes are sold, revenue share, demand).
- There is no data on Notion-website template sales.
- Notion's eligible-country list could not be read.

## 4. Canva website templates (Canva Creators; Etsy)

### Takeaway
The official Canva Creators program is a beta with long application waits. It pays usage-based royalties at undisclosed rates, through Trolley via bank, Payoneer or PayPal. Taiwan eligibility is unconfirmed. Selling Canva template links on Etsy works anywhere Etsy pays out, but it is a crowded, low-price market, and I found no evidence specific to Canva *website* templates. Verdict: NO-GO as a main channel; at most a cheap experiment.

### Cited Findings
- **Program:**
  - Canva Creators reach more than 85M users in 190 countries and are paid royalties based on usage.
  - The Template Creator route requires a portfolio application; it is "in beta" and an answer can take a couple of months.
  - Element Creator signups are on hold, and Contributor signups are paused.
  - Canva does not publish royalty rates.
  - A new contributor agreement took effect January 14, 2026.
  - Sources: [Canva Help: Canva Creators program](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-creators-program/); [Krea, Sept 2026](https://www.krea.ai/blog/can-you-make-money-with-canva-creators-program-and-etsy-templates-in-2026); [Canva contributor agreement update](https://www.canva.com/sk_sk/help/updates-to-contributor-agreement-jan-2026/)
- **Payouts:** by bank (including Payoneer), paid in the currency of the bank-account country, or by PayPal in USD. The search summary says payouts run through Trolley. — [Canva Help: Setting up payments](https://www.canva.com/help/set-up-payout-details/). I found no confirmation that Taiwan is a supported bank-account country.
- **Etsy and Canva links:**
  - Templates that use Pro content can only be sold as Canva template links.
  - Designs must be original; you cannot resell existing Canva templates.
  - Etsy fees are $0.20 per listing plus 6.5% per transaction; sellers keep about 85–93% before ads.
  - Source: [Krea 2026](https://www.krea.ai/blog/can-you-make-money-with-canva-creators-program-and-etsy-templates-in-2026)
- **Earnings (blog figures, low reliability):**
  - New sellers: under $500/month in year 1, and $0–200/month in the first 60 days.
  - Established shops: $500–2,000/month.
  - Top sellers: $3–20k/month, a small slice of sellers.
  - Sources: [biztoolkit 2026](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/how-much-do-canva-template-sellers-make-in-2026); [litcommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/selling-templates-on-etsy/); [decidenett, Oct 2026](https://decidenett.blogspot.com/2026/10/selling-canva-templates-etsy-creative-market-guide.html)
  - Social media, résumé and wedding-invitation templates are the most crowded categories.

### Inferences
- Canva websites are a simple one-page format with no real language switcher, so the trilingual and motion strengths are lost.
- Etsy prices for Canva templates are usually far below $39.
- A low-effort side test is possible: wedding or event one-pagers and menus are where the seller's existing wedding and restaurant designs overlap with what Canva buyers already purchase.

### Gaps
- There is no data specific to Canva *website* templates (demand, prices).
- Taiwan's Canva Creators payout eligibility is unconfirmed; check Settings > Payments in a Canva account.

## 5. Competition, year-1 revenue, test strategy, and own-store vs marketplace

### Takeaway
All three website-builder marketplaces became crowded and saw falling sales in 2026: Framer opened its gates, Webflow sales fell, and AI site generators undercut templates. The lowest-risk path is to rebuild 3–5 best-fit templates in Framer only. Sell them as remix links through the seller's existing Gumroad, which pays out to Taiwan, and list on the Framer Marketplace only once payout eligibility is confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Framer has more than 7,000 creators and about 10.3k templates; Webflow has more than 7,000 templates. — [Framer 3 blog](https://www.framer.com/blog/framer-3/); [zoyaqib.com](https://www.zoyaqib.com/post/september-2026-update-is-it-still-worth-selling-webflow-templates-in-2026)
- Gumroad pays out to bank accounts in Taiwan in TWD. — [Gumroad Help: Getting paid](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid) (per search summary)
- Framer and Webflow templates can both be delivered off-platform: Framer through remix links, Webflow through fulfillment links that keep the 95% terms. — [Contra](https://contra.com/sell-framer-templates); [Webflow updates](https://webflow.com/updates/template-creator-enhancements)

### Inferences
- **Year-1 for a new creator, my estimate based on the figures above:**
  - Framer: about $0–1,000/month with 5–10 templates, heavily dependent on marketing on X or Dribbble and on Framer featuring.
  - Webflow: lower.
  - Canva/Etsy: under $500/month.
- **Best-fit templates to convert first:**
  - SaaS landing (largest Framer category)
  - portfolio
  - one Motion edition (shows off Framer animation)
  - one niche SMB template such as restaurant or clinic, to test the under-served local-business niche
  - wedding as a possible Canva crossover
- **Test design:** rebuild 3 templates in Framer, sell remix links on Gumroad as a $39–69 add-on or bundle upsell to existing buyers, then measure for 60–90 days before expanding.

### Gaps
- There is no verified post-June-2026 sales data for new Framer creators.
- The seller's own Gumroad conversion data is needed for a baseline.

## 6. Can a Taiwan individual get paid? (Stripe)

### Takeaway
Stripe does not let Taiwan-based businesses open accounts. That blocks or complicates every Stripe-based marketplace payout: Framer (likely), Webflow and Notion. Stripe's Global Payouts list does include Taiwan, but whether a given platform uses it is that platform's choice. The workarounds are a US LLC with a US bank account, or merchant-of-record and Gumroad routes.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe is not available for Taiwan businesses. Stripe Atlas reportedly cannot form businesses for Taiwan, while Stripe's own page says Atlas works "no matter what country/region you're based in". The two conflict; confirm with Stripe. — [persuasion-nation](https://persuasion-nation.com/is-stripe-available-in-taiwan/); [stripe.com/global](https://stripe.com/fr-bg/global)
- A Taiwanese developer has run Stripe through a $139 Wyoming LLC for two years (anecdotal; carries US filing duties). — [yu-wenhao.com](https://yu-wenhao.com/en/blog/non-us-resident-llc-guide/)
- Stripe Global Payouts lists Taiwan (TWD, wire). — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/global-payouts/recipient-countries)
- Stripe Connect does not offer self-serve cross-border payouts outside its listed regions. — [Stripe cross-border payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts)
- Taiwan tax: foreign sellers of electronic services must register for VAT once annual sales to Taiwan individuals exceed TWD 600,000. — [Stripe Tax Taiwan](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/supported-countries/asia-pacific/taiwan)
- Merchant-of-record services (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) do not require your own Stripe account. — [alignlabs.dev](https://alignlabs.dev/guides/stripe-alternatives-for-non-us-companies)

### Inferences
- Before any rebuild, email Framer, Webflow and Notion support and ask: "Can an individual resident of Taiwan receive marketplace payouts?"
- A US LLC is a fallback with real ongoing costs.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Taiwan is on Stripe Connect's cross-border payout recipient list for US platforms (Framer and Webflow are US-registered), because the docs could not be fetched.
