# Customer Segments (Freelancers/Agencies, Overseas Chinese SMBs, Nonprofits/Churches) and Payout Platforms for a Taiwan-Based Solo Template Seller

> Research note (2026-11-22). Method limitation: page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked for every domain tried (gumroad.com, docs.lemonsqueezy.com, shareuhack.com), so **all findings below come from search-engine result summaries/snippets, not full-page reads**. ## 1. Freelancers / small design agencies as buyers (bundles, agency licences, where they hang out, what marketing works)

### Takeaway
Freelancers and agencies do buy templates, but the low end of the market is dominated by subscription "unlimited download" libraries (Envato Elements ~US$11.50–33/month), so a US$199 bundle or US$119 10-site licence must be sold on *differentiation* (trilingual EN/繁中/简中 + switcher, vertical-specific small-business layouts), not on price per template. Generic template listings on Gumroad are saturated and sales are heavily concentrated: distribution, not product, is the bottleneck.

### Cited Findings
**Competing price/licence models**
- Envato Elements individual plan cited at US$16.50/month or US$138/year (US$11.50/month); Teams tier US$10.75/month per member; custom Enterprise — [CreatorsRes / Envato reviews via search](https://creatorsres.com/envato-elements-review/); another review says monthly billing runs ~US$33/month — [Red11 Media](https://www.red11media.com/blog/envato-elements-explained); a 2026 pricing guide lists a higher "Ultimate" tier at US$109 (annual) for high-volume work — [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/envato/pricing). (Figures conflict across sources; verify on envato.com.)
- Elements licensing: one source says a single commercial licence covers personal and client work; another says each asset must be registered/licensed per project — conflicting, check current Envato licence terms — [Arahoster](https://blog.arahoster.com/envato-elements-in-depth-the-ultimate-creative-subscription-explained/); [FreeVisuals](https://www.freevisuals.net/post/envato-elements-review).
- Envato Market (ThemeForest) is pay-per-item; extended licences cited at US$100–500+ per asset — [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/envato).
- Creative Market: pay-per-item, no subscription; no formal team pricing, bulk deals negotiated directly with authors — [Getly / Vendr summaries](https://www.getly.store/alternatives/creative-market).
- Creative Market seller economics for template makers (2026 breakdown) — [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/creative-market-marketplace-breakdown/).

**Gumroad template market evidence**
- Gumroad has an official help article specifically for buying/selling HTML templates (category is supported) — [Gumroad Help](https://help.gumroad.com/article/310-sell-html-templates).
- Gumroad charges a 10% flat fee per sale (plus card/PayPal processing), no monthly fee. Gumroad company revenue 2025 reported at US$17.8M (down ~6% YoY), net income US$3.8M (from SEC filing analysis) — [everything-pr.com](https://everything-pr.com/gumroad-the-digital-product-marketplace-of-the-indie-creator-economy).
- Saturation/concentration: in a sampled Gumroad HTML-template category, 70% of 44 visible listings had no rating, and the top 10% of products collected 96.6% of the category's ratings — [stealwhatworks.com](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/gumroad-products-making-real-money-now) (third-party analysis, small sample).
- Anecdotes (flag: anecdotal, self-reported): a seller of five Next.js/landing-page templates got ~92 visits, 10 free downloads, US$0 sales and concluded "it is a distribution problem, not conversion" — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/next-js-templates-months-of-work-0-in-revenue-here-is-what-i-got-wrong-25d7012907); a freelance web designer reportedly reached US$10k selling website templates — [moneyanddebt.com 2025 guide](https://moneyanddebt.com/p/selling-digital-products-on-gumroad-guide-for-creators/); a first-month Gumroad shop made US$487, later US$400–600/month — [Medium, Dylan Ryder](https://dylan-ryder.medium.com/from-0-to-500-my-first-month-selling-digital-products-on-gumroad-116eb4f43581); US$9,400 selling Google Stitch templates — [Medium](https://medium.com/the-money-guide/how-i-made-9-400-selling-google-stitch-templates-on-gumroad-cfc066391f88).
- Marketing: "Twitter seems to be a big driver for Gumroad stores, much more than Instagram"; recommended approach = one template, one narrow buyer group, publish practical examples around the job the template solves — [lowcontentprofits.com](https://lowcontentprofits.com/gumroad-digital-products-earnings/); [stealwhatworks.com](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/gumroad-products-making-real-money-now).
- "Building in public" with no audience yields little early revenue (anecdote) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/what-0-revenue-after-2-weeks-of-building-in-public-actually-teaches-you-40fa009998).

### Inferences
- The seller's US$119 "10 sites" extended licence sits between ThemeForest extended licences (US$100–500+/item) and Envato Elements' unlimited subscription; for agencies the natural upsell is an *unlimited-sites / agency* tier on the US$199 bundle (e.g. US$299–399) since agencies compare against an Elements subscription that costs ~US$140–400/year.
- The trilingual (繁/简/EN) switcher is a genuine gap vs. Envato/Creative Market stock, which is overwhelmingly English-only — the most defensible positioning for freelancers serving Chinese-speaking clients (Taiwan, HK, Malaysia, Singapore, diaspora agencies).
- Given the 96.6% concentration, marketplace listing alone is unlikely to work; demos/case-study posts on X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, and a Product Hunt launch for the bundle are the evidence-backed channels. Subreddit self-promotion rules (r/webdev, r/web_design) usually restrict direct promotion — unverified in this session.
- UI8 (curated, design-file focused, application-based) — unverified in this session.

### Gaps
- No primary data found on UI8 pricing/licence, Lemon Squeezy creator template earnings, or Product Hunt/Designer News/Reddit template launch results in 2025–2026 (search returned nothing specific).
- No quantitative data on what share of template buyers are agencies vs. end-business owners.

## 2. Overseas Chinese small businesses (US/CA/AU/NZ/UK): demand for bilingual sites, current suppliers and prices, channels

### Takeaway
Local diaspora web shops already sell bilingual Chinese/English sites in roughly the CA$599–1,599 range (Toronto), and restaurant-ordering SaaS undercuts at ~CA$350/year — so the seller's US$249–449 customisation service is price-competitive, and a trilingual (繁+简+EN) template is a differentiator. Xiaohongshu (RED) is the most evidenced discovery channel for this segment (10M+ overseas users; US/CA/AU/UK core markets).

### Cited Findings
- Toronto/GTA: 火烈鸟科技 (Flamingo Techs) prices CA$599 / CA$799 / CA$1,599 (+tax), fastest 5 business days; the CA$799 tier is the 中英双语 (bilingual) plan incl. business email; redesign/custom dev CA$500–4,000+; targets restaurants/takeout, renovation, real estate, law firms in Markham, Richmond Hill, North York, Scarborough — [flamingotechs.com](https://www.flamingotechs.com/locations/toronto-web-design).
- Vancouver: Goopter markets bilingual/multilingual restaurant online-ordering sites + QR ordering from US$/CA$350/year, no commission, live in 1–2 days (forum promo post; verify) — [ComeFromChina forum](https://bbs.comefromchina.com/threads/1706924/).
- Seattle Chinese portal carries classified posts offering commission-free restaurant ordering websites (no price) — evidence that 华人资讯网 classifieds are a live channel for web services — [seattlechinaren.com](https://www.seattlechinaren.com/f/page_viewtopic/t_41413.html).
- Mainstream benchmark: restaurant website builders (Wix/Squarespace) ~US$16–49/month; custom multi-location restaurant builds US$8,000–25,000+ — [Webyking](https://www.webyking.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-a-restaurant-website/).
- Xiaohongshu overseas users >10M across 44 countries/regions; core markets US, Canada, Australia, UK, Singapore, Malaysia; users mainly 20–45 (students, new immigrants) — [jizhil.com](https://www.jizhil.com/xhsdata/5945.html) (marketing-industry source, figures unaudited).
- ABC (Australia) reports RED's deep influence on the Chinese diaspora in Australia (2023, older) — [ABC News 中文](https://www.abc.net.au/chinese/2023-12-12/how-super-app-red-influences-chinese-diaspora-in-australia/103218562).
- RED announced an e-commerce overseas expansion plan (first phase US, HK, Macau; in beta) — [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1888528391969428193); [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/it/6200865.html).
- Australian agencies sell 小红书代运营 to local Chinese merchants; advice: build 3 months of organic content before paid boosting — [zipzipe.com.au](https://www.zipzipe.com.au/zh-hans/blog/xiaohongshu-marketing/); [Alive Tech](https://www.alivetech.com.au/xiaohongshu/).

### Inferences
- Positioning: "繁/简/EN in one site" serves both Cantonese/HK/Taiwan-origin and Mainland-origin customers — a concrete selling point vs. agencies offering 中英 only.
- The seller's US$249–449 service (~CA$345–620) undercuts Flamingo's CA$799 bilingual tier while delivering trilingual; a "done-for-you bilingual site" package is likely more sellable to this segment than a raw template (owners of restaurants/clinics rarely self-edit HTML).
- Channels to test: Xiaohongshu posts showing before/after sites for diaspora restaurants/clinics; classifieds on 51.ca, ComeFromChina, 华人资讯网 (Seattle), 1point3acres-type portals; Chinese chambers of commerce newsletters/sponsorship; Yelp-listed Chinese businesses without websites as cold-outreach targets. (Channel effectiveness unverified.)

## 3. Nonprofits / churches: needs, budgets, cheap alternatives, templates vs services, reach

### Takeaway
Budgets are tight (59% of US nonprofits <US$50k/yr; 52% have ≤3 months cash), and they have heavily discounted SaaS options (Wix 70% off via TechSoup; church platforms from ~US$19/month). Their pain is ongoing maintenance, giving/sermon features and non-technical staff, so a static HTML template is a weak fit; a low-cost setup service (or a bilingual/trilingual site for immigrant Chinese churches) is the more plausible product.

### Cited Findings
- 59% of US nonprofits have annual budgets under US$50,000 (Zeffy citing National Council of Nonprofits) — [Zeffy](https://www.zeffy.com/blog/nonprofit-statistics).
- NFF 2025 survey (2,206 orgs): 52% have ≤3 months of cash; costs rising faster than funding — [Nonprofit Finance Fund](https://nff.org/state-of-the-nonprofit-sector-survey/2025-state-of-the-survey-nonprofit-sector-survey/).
- Website cost estimates (vendor blogs, rough): small charities US$1,500–4,000 (median ~US$2,750) — [projectcostestimator.com](https://projectcostestimator.com/nonprofit-website-cost); US$3,000–12,000 for a simple professional site — [HyperArts](https://www.hyperarts.com/nonprofit-website-cost/); rule of thumb 2–5% of operating budget in year one, 1–2% after — [Kris Toney Design](https://kristoney.me/budget-for-a-website-2025/).
- Wix: 70% discount on Premium plans for eligible nonprofits via TechSoup (must be TechSoup members) — [Wix non-profit page](https://www.wix.com/non-profit) (a third-party review says 50%; official figure is 70%).
- Squarespace nonprofit discount: conflicting (10% promo, 20% with 501(c)(3) proof, or no official discount) — [websitebuilderinsider](https://www.websitebuilderinsider.com/does-squarespace-have-nonprofit-pricing/); [Squarestyle guide](https://squarestyle.squarespace.com/tutorials/squarespace-nonprofit-pricing-guide). Unverified.
- Google for Nonprofits: free Workspace edition requires registration as a charity in an eligible country and Goodstack verification — [Google Support](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/2858465). (Website builder not included as such; Taiwan eligibility not confirmed in results.)
- Church platforms (2026 comparison blogs): Tithe.ly Sites ~US$19/month (one source lists a US$149 setup fee, another none; 30-day trial); Nucleus Essential US$65 / Standard US$99 / Complete US$199 per month (as of Sept 2026 per one source; others cite US$39–129) ; The Church Co US$29–199/month; Clover Sites quote-only; Squarespace "no church features out of the box" — [ReachRight Studios](https://reachrightstudios.com/blog/church-website-builders/); [ReachRight cost guide](https://reachrightstudios.com/blog/church-website-cost/); [churchdigital.com](https://churchdigital.com/website-builders). Note: these blogs are often published by competing church web agencies.

### Inferences
- Static HTML templates lack giving, sermon archive, events CMS — the features church platforms sell — so the template product mainly fits very small orgs/volunteer webmasters wanting a free-hosted (GitHub Pages/Netlify) brochure site with zero monthly fee; that "no subscription" angle vs. US$19–199/month SaaS is the pitch.
- Niche with fit: overseas Chinese churches and Taiwanese/HK diaspora associations needing 繁/简/EN pages (overlaps segment 2).
- Ekklesia360 pricing not found; likely quote-based (unverified).
- Overall profitability likely lowest of the three segments given budgets and free/discounted alternatives.

### Gaps
- No data on whether nonprofits buy HTML templates vs. services; no Ekklesia360 price; no verified Squarespace discount; no reach-channel evidence (e.g., TechSoup marketplace, denominational networks) gathered.

## 4. Payouts and selling platforms for a Taiwan individual (2025–2026) + tax basics

### Takeaway
Gumroad (merchant of record since 1 Jan 2025, pays out to Taiwan) remains the simplest default; Lemon Squeezy and Creative Market likely work via PayPal; Etsy is effectively closed to Taiwan sellers (not an Etsy Payments country); Stripe direct accounts are not a reliable route for a Taiwan individual. Taiwan tax: income from services/products performed in Taiwan is arguably Taiwan-source income for 綜所稅 — not merely "overseas income" — so consult an accountant.

### Cited Findings
**Gumroad**
- Official help: Gumroad pays by direct bank deposit in local currency for most countries, PayPal only where bank deposits aren't available; no Payoneer, Wise, checks or wires — [Gumroad Help: Getting paid](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid); [Payoneer and Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/help/article/223-payoneer-and-gumroad).
- Taiwan specifics (secondary): Taiwan payout options = bank (SWIFT) or PayPal; TWD payout with a TWD 800 minimum mentioned; Taiwan banks charge inbound SWIFT fees; PayPal withdrawals to Taiwan carry FX spread/withdrawal fee — [ShareUHack 2026 comparison](https://www.shareuhack.com/en/posts/digital-product-platform-comparison-asia-2026); [ShareUHack Taiwan guide](https://www.shareuhack.com/posts/taiwan-creator-digital-product-selling-guide-2026); [site-now.app Gumroad Taiwan guide 2026](https://site-now.app/gumroad-taiwan-guide/). (Could not open these pages; snippets only.)
- PayPal–Gumroad disruption in Oct/Q4 2024; one source says partnership restored early 2025 — check per country — [ThriveCart article](https://thrivecart.com/?p=29700).
- Gumroad acts as Merchant of Record handling global sales tax from 1 Jan 2025; creator still must report income; Gumroad issues no Taiwan tax certificates/invoices — use sales reports — [Taiwan indie hacker payment guide](https://indiehackerstaiwan.com/official/taiwan-indie-hacker-payment-guide); [deanlife.blog](https://deanlife.blog/gumroad-tutorial/).
- Fee: 10% flat per sale + processing — [everything-pr.com](https://everything-pr.com/gumroad-the-digital-product-marketplace-of-the-indie-creator-economy).

**Lemon Squeezy**
- Official supported-countries page: PayPal payouts in 200+ countries/regions plus bank payouts in a list of ~79 countries — [Lemon Squeezy docs](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries); [LS blog: bank payouts 79 countries](https://lemonsqueezy.com/blog/new-bank-payouts). Whether Taiwan is on the *bank* list could not be confirmed (snippet truncated); PayPal route is the likely path.

**Stripe**
- Stripe Tax supports Taiwan for remote sellers of digital goods (Oct 2025 changelog) — this is about collecting Taiwan VAT, not a Taiwan merchant account — [Stripe changelog](https://docs.stripe.com/changelog/clover/2025-10-29/stripe-tax-taiwan-remote-support).
- Taiwan-resident direct Stripe accounts: conflicting secondary claims ("not available" vs. "can apply but payout leg is the problem; reliable path is a US company / Stripe Atlas") — [IncorpUK](https://incorpuk.com/blog/how-to-open-stripe-account-in-taiwan/) (commercial bias); [ShareUHack dev guide](https://www.shareuhack.com/en/posts/taiwan-indie-dev-global-payments-guide-2026).

**Etsy**
- Taiwan not on Etsy Payments eligible list; Etsy's Payoneer-route list includes China, Japan, South Korea, Thailand but not Taiwan — [Etsy Help: Countries eligible for Etsy Payments](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015710408); [Etsy Help: Payoneer with Etsy Payments](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/16999319005207).
- Secondary claim: Taiwan seller accounts closed in Aug 2025; as of Apr 2026 still unsupported — [ShareUHack Etsy guide](https://www.shareuhack.com/en/posts/etsy-digital-product-taiwan-creator-guide-2026) (not verified against Etsy).

**Creative Market**
- Payout methods: PayPal (free US; non-US 1% up to US$10), ACH/eCheck, wire; payouts via Tipalti; PayPal must be in a "receivable" country — [Creative Market: Taxes & Payouts](https://support.creativemarket.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004015634-Shop-Owner-Guide-to-Taxes-Payouts); [Which payout methods in my country](https://support.creativemarket.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004067573-Which-payout-methods-are-available-in-my-country).
- Minimum payout conflicting: US$20 monthly request window (Freemius 2026) vs US$50 twice monthly (2025 blog) — [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/creative-market-marketplace-breakdown/); [ladybossincome](https://ladybossincome.com/set-up-a-creative-market-shop/).
- Shop opening: one 2025 guide says click "Open a Shop" with a PayPal account; older source says invitation/application required — [ladybossincome](https://ladybossincome.com/set-up-a-creative-market-shop/); [MakeUseOf](https://www.makeuseof.com/what-is-creative-market/) (older).

**Fiverr / Upwork**
- Fiverr withdrawal options: PayPal (no fee, US$1 min), Payoneer bank transfer (US$1 fee, US$20 min), Payoneer account, Fiverr Revenue Card — [Fiverr Help](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010530058-Withdrawing-your-earnings-managing-payout-methods). Taiwan-specific availability not shown.
- Upwork: Payoneer is the official partner, $0 to withdraw to Payoneer; Wise USD also cited $0 — [feebite](https://feebite.com/blog/upwork-payment-methods-compared).

**PayPal / Payoneer / Wise in Taiwan**
- PayPal Taiwan publishes a dedicated withdrawal process for Taiwan customers (withdraw to local bank) — [PayPal TW withdrawal process](https://www.paypal.com/tw/webapps/mpp/withdrawal-process); [PayPal TW FAQ](https://paypal.com/tw/smarthelp/article/faq2726).
- Payoneer: secondary sources say Taiwan users can receive from marketplaces and withdraw to local banks; one Taiwanese user blog reports ~1.2% withdrawal fee plus unpredictable intermediary-bank charges (anecdotal, possibly dated) — [OneSafe](https://www.onesafe.io/blog/does-payoneer-work-in-taiwan); [vocus.cc](https://vocus.cc/article/5ed71160fd89780001e65852); WorldFirst markets itself as a Payoneer alternative in Taiwan — [WorldFirst TW](https://www.worldfirst.com/tw/blog/online-sellers/payoneer-taiwan-fees-alternatives/).
- Wise: TWD cannot be held as a balance; Taiwan-resident Wise account eligibility to receive USD not confirmed — [Wise MY](https://wise.com/my/blog/use-wise-card-taiwan); [Wise Taiwan payment methods](https://wise.com/gb/hub/payment-methods/taiwan).

**Taiwan tax basics (consult an accountant / 國稅局)**
- 海外所得 (overseas income) ≥ NT$1,000,000/yr per household must be included in the 最低稅負 (AMT) filing; basic tax payable only above the basic exemption (NT$6.7M cited by articles; Kaohsiung NTB notice for tax year 113 cites NT$7.5M — conflict, use current MOF figure) — [UDN 2025](https://money.udn.com/money/story/6710/8780236); [財政部](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/384fb3077bb349ea973e7fc6f13b6974?cntId=27267b48640a45ee8cabb6243f62616d).
- Key nuance: work performed in Taiwan (selling self-made products/services from Taiwan) is in principle 中華民國來源所得 and goes into regular 綜合所得稅, not merely "overseas income" — contradicting some Gumroad tutorials that say income under NT$1M is untaxed — [Taiwan indie hacker payment guide](https://indiehackerstaiwan.com/official/taiwan-indie-hacker-payment-guide); [LY CPA on vocus](https://vocus.cc/article/6af72cb1fd89780001b5d2d1).
- Inbound remittance records to Taiwan bank accounts are visible to the tax bureau; overseas income won't appear on the NTB pre-filled income list — the taxpayer must self-report — [Taiwan indie hacker guide](https://indiehackerstaiwan.com/official/taiwan-indie-hacker-payment-guide); [UDN](https://money.udn.com/money/story/6710/8780236).

### Inferences
- Practical stack for a Taiwan individual: Gumroad (MoR, handles VAT) as primary storefront → payout to Taiwan bank or PayPal; Lemon Squeezy (also MoR) as alternative via PayPal; Creative Market as additional marketplace via PayPal; Fiverr for the customization service (PayPal/Payoneer). Skip Etsy. Avoid direct Stripe unless forming a US LLC (adds US compliance costs).
- Customisation service sold directly (invoice) to overseas clients is likely classified differently (service income, possibly 執行業務/營利 depending on setup); if volume grows, registering a business (行號/公司) and 營業稅 questions arise — accountant needed.
- Unverified background: Lemon Squeezy fee 5% + US$0.50; Gumroad also adds US$0.50/transaction and 30% fee on Discover-marketplace sales; Payhip 5% (free plan) and pays via PayPal/Stripe; Fiverr takes 20% of seller earnings. Verify on official pages.

### Gaps
- Could not open official Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy pages (network blocked) to confirm Taiwan is on bank-payout lists or the TWD 800 minimum.
- No search evidence for Payhip payouts to Taiwan, UI8 seller eligibility, Upwork Taiwan payout methods, or Wise Business for Taiwan residents.
- No 2026 MOF primary page on taxation of foreign-platform digital sales for individuals; AMT threshold figures conflict (NT$6.7M vs NT$7.5M).
