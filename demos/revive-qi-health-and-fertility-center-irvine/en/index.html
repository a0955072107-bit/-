Your first visit and FAQ

Coming in for the first time? Here is what helps your visit go smoothly.

What to bring

  • Insurance cardIf you have one, so we can check your coverage before your visit.
  • Photo IDA parent or guardian should come with children.
  • A list of current medicationsIncluding blood thinners, supplements and herbal products.
  • Medical history and allergiesHeart conditions, diabetes, pregnancy or drug allergies.
  • Arrive 10 minutes earlyTo fill in a new patient form.

Frequently asked questions

Does insurance cover acupuncture?

Some health plans cover acupuncture. Call us with the name of your plan and we will help you check.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing that can be rolled up to the knee and elbow.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on your condition and how you respond. We talk about a plan after your first consultation.

Can I keep taking my regular medication?

Tell us everything you take, including supplements, and keep following your doctor’s advice.

What if I can’t make my appointment?

Please call us as early as you can so we can find you a new time.
