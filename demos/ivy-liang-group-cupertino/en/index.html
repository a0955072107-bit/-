How buying works

Five steps from the first call to the keys.

  1. Talk it through

    We go over your budget, timing and the neighborhoods you like.

  2. Get pre-approved

    A lender reviews your finances so you know your price range.

  3. Tour homes

    We set up showings and look at condition, location and HOA fees together.

  4. Make an offer

    With recent sales in hand, we write and negotiate your offer.

  5. Close and move in

    Inspection, appraisal and title are scheduled, then you sign and get the keys.
