Frequently asked questions

How do I know which class is right for my child?

Call us and tell us your child’s grade and what they need help with. We will suggest a class and a time to come in.

Can we visit before signing up?

Of course. Call ahead so someone is free to show you around and answer your questions.

How much is tuition?

It depends on the class and schedule. Call the front desk and we will go over the options with you.

Can I talk to you in Chinese?

This website is in English, 繁體中文 and 简体中文. Call us and ask for the language you prefer.

Questions about classes?

Call us during front desk hours. We are happy to help you find the right fit.
