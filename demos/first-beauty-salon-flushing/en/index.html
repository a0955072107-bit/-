Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is the best way to get the time you want. Call us to check today’s availability.

How long does a color or perm take?

Most color and perm services take about two to three hours, depending on hair length and the look you want.

How do I keep my color from fading?

Wash with lukewarm water, use a color-safe shampoo and protect your hair from strong sun and heat tools.

I’m not sure what style suits me. Can you help?

Of course. Bring a few photos you like, and your stylist will suggest options for your face shape and hair type.
