Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Call us at (718) 301-0158.

Sample questions: we will replace these with the ones your clients ask most.

What should I bring for my tax return?

A photo ID, last year’s return and this year’s forms, such as W-2s, 1099s and records of deductions. Call us and we can tell you what else to bring.

How much does tax preparation cost?

It depends on your situation and the forms involved. We tell you the fee before we start.

I received a letter from the IRS. What should I do?

Do not ignore it. Bring or send us the letter and we can explain what it means and what the next step is.

What if I missed the tax deadline?

Call us as soon as you can. We will explain your options for filing.
