Services

After an exam, your dentist explains the treatment options that suit your needs and what they cost.

General Dentistry

Checkups, cleanings, fillings and root canal treatment. Good oral health starts with everyday care.

Children’s Dentistry

Baby teeth checkups, fluoride varnish and sealants, explained in words kids understand so they get comfortable at the dentist.

Gum Care

If your gums bleed or a tooth feels loose, we can check your gum health and plan treatment and follow-up visits to match.

Dental Implants

We use imaging to assess bone and overall health first, then explain the process, timeline and aftercare before scheduling.

Orthodontics

Consultations for traditional braces and clear aligners. We discuss the options for your bite and an estimated treatment time.

Teeth Whitening

We check your teeth and gums first, then explain the difference between in-office and at-home whitening and what to keep in mind.

Your first visit and FAQ

Coming in for the first time? Here is what helps your visit go smoothly.

What to bring

  • Insurance cardSo we can check your coverage. No insurance? Just let us know when you book.
  • Photo IDUsed to set up your patient record. A parent or guardian should come with children.
  • A list of current medicationsPlease tell your dentist about blood thinners, osteoporosis medication and similar.
  • Medical history and allergiesHeart conditions, diabetes, pregnancy or drug allergies.
  • Arrive 10 minutes earlyTo fill in a new patient form so your dentist understands your needs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead by phone or WhatsApp usually means a shorter wait. Walk-in patients are seen in order of arrival.

How often should I have a cleaning? Does insurance cover it?

Many adults come in every six months, and many dental plans cover two cleanings a year. Your dentist will recommend a schedule based on your exam.

When should my child first see a dentist?

We suggest starting regular checkups once the first baby tooth comes in. Fluoride varnish can be applied at these visits if your dentist recommends it.

How much do implants or braces cost?

Costs depend on your oral health and the treatment you choose. After an exam, your dentist will go over a treatment plan and itemized estimate before anything begins.

What if I can’t make my appointment?

Please call or message us on WhatsApp as early as you can. We will help you reschedule, and the time can go to another patient who needs it.
