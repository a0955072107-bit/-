Questions

How long does color or a perm take?

Color takes 2 to 3 hours and a perm 3 to 4. Bleach adds more. We’ll tell you the likely finish time when you book.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Message us on WhatsApp by the day before. After two same-day cancellations, we ask for a deposit on future bookings.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids 12 and under get the kids’ price. Weekday afternoons are the quietest time to come.

Can I come in just to ask questions?

Yes. If you’d like to think it over after the chat, there’s no charge.
