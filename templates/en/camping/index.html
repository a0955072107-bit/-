Camp amenities

Nights up here are quiet. We take care of the small stuff so you can enjoy them.

  • Hot showers

    Twelve private stalls with hot water around the clock.

  • Site power

    30-amp hookups, enough for a heated blanket or a kettle.

  • Wi-Fi

    At the camp store only. The sites stay happily offline.

  • Dog friendly

    Leashed dogs welcome, plus a dog wash and an off-leash meadow.

  • Dish stations

    Three wash-up sinks with soap and compost bins.

  • Community fire

    Lit nightly 7:00–9:30 pm. Firewood sold at the store.

  • Fridge and ice

    Shared fridges for your food, bags of ice for $3.

  • Gear rental

    Tent, sleeping bags and chairs for $55. Show up and sleep.

Camp rules

Sound carries a long way in the valley. A few simple rules keep everyone sleeping well.

Quiet hours
  • Quiet hours run 10:00 pm–7:00 am. Speakers off, voices low.
  • No starting engines or running generators overnight.
  • Repeat issues mean we will ask you to leave without a refund.
Pets
  • Dogs are welcome on a leash and stay at your site overnight.
  • Please pick up after them. Free bags at the camp store.
  • One pet per glamping tent. Let us know when you book.
Trash
  • Sort trash, recycling and compost at the station by the camp store.
  • Bottle cooking grease and hand it in. Never pour it down a sink.
  • Leave your site as you found it. We walk the sites at 11 am.
Fires
  • Use a raised fire pit only. No fires directly on the grass.
  • Fire bans apply in high wind or dry spells. Check the board.
  • Drown your fire before bed and dump ashes in the ash can.
Check-in and check-out
  • Check-in from 1 pm, check-out by 11 am. Early or late times depend on availability and cost extra.
  • The road gets dark after 7 pm, so try to arrive before nightfall.

Getting up the mountain

About 40 minutes from the highway. The last stretch is a mountain road, so give yourself time.

By car

  1. Take US-24 west to Juniper Falls
  2. Turn onto County Road 12 at the general store
  3. Follow the Starfield Camp signs right at the fork
  4. Climb about 4 miles of gravel to the gate

Mountain road notes

  • The last 4 miles are single-lane with tight switchbacks. Uphill traffic has the right of way.
  • Cell service drops out 1 mile before camp. Download offline maps first.
  • RVs over 23 feet, please call ahead.
  • There are no streetlights, so aim to arrive before 6 pm.

Without a car

Take the regional mountain bus to the Juniper Falls stop. We run a shuttle by reservation for $40 per trip. Just add it to your booking notes.
