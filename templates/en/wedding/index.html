Our Story

From one borrowed roll of film to a lifetime of adventures together.

  1. 2016

    We met at the campus photo club

    Daniel showed up with a vintage camera and no film. Emma had a spare roll. When it came back from the lab, the very first frame was her.

  2. 2019

    Our first road trip

    Five days down the Pacific Coast Highway in a car with no air conditioning. We got lost twice, ate too many tacos and learned we make a great team.

  3. 2023

    Our first home together

    A creaky old apartment with huge windows. We painted every wall ourselves and adopted an olive tree that still refuses to thrive.

  4. 2026

    The proposal at Mount Tabor

    Daniel said he wanted to shoot the sunset. His camera held a fresh roll of film and a ring. Emma said yes just as the sun dipped below the hills.

Questions

Can I bring my kids?

Absolutely, little ones are welcome. Please include them in your guest count and add their ages in your note so we can set up high chairs and kids’ meals.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes. Free parking is available in the estate’s lower lot, and rideshare drop-off is at the main gate. The hotel shuttle is the easiest option if you plan to raise a glass.

Do you have a registry?

Your presence is the best gift. If you’d like to give something, our registry is on Zola, and there will be a card box at the reception.

What should I wear?

Cocktail attire. The ceremony is on grass, so skip the stilettos. May evenings in Portland get cool, so bring a light layer.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We’re having an unplugged ceremony, so please keep phones tucked away and let our photographer do the work. During cocktails and the reception, snap away and send us your favorites.
