01

Our classes

Six formats covering strength, conditioning and mobility. Every class is graded by level, so beginners can start with confidence.

01

Strength Training

Built around the squat, deadlift and bench press. Your coach fine-tunes every rep so you build strength safely.

  • Intermediate
  • 60 min
  • Max 8 people
02

HIIT

Short, intense circuits that boost your conditioning and burn serious energy. The 45 minutes after work you will feel the most.

  • Advanced
  • 45 min
  • Max 12 people
03

Boxing Conditioning

Footwork, combinations and pad work. Blow off steam while you build core strength and coordination.

  • Intermediate
  • 60 min
  • Gloves provided
04

Stretch Yoga

Focused on breath and joint mobility to help you recover after training and loosen the neck and shoulders that a desk job tightens.

  • Beginner
  • 60 min
  • Mats provided
05

TRX Suspension

Use your own bodyweight and suspension straps to build core stability. Change the angle to make it easier or harder.

  • Beginner
  • 50 min
  • Max 10 people
06

Personal Training

We start with a posture and movement assessment, then build a program around you. Ideal if you have a clear goal or an old injury to work around.

  • All levels
  • 60 min
  • By appointment

Meet the coaches

Every coach holds a recognized certification and keeps learning, so you make progress safely with people who know their craft.

Leo Carter

Head coach, strength and body composition

A former college weightlifter who specializes in strength and physique work. Patient, detail-oriented and big on movement quality.

  • NSCA-CSCS
  • Injury prevention
  • 10 years coaching

Kai Brooks

HIIT and TRX coach

Fast-paced, high-energy classes. Kai specializes in functional training and fat-loss programming.

  • ACE-CPT
  • TRX STC
  • CPR/AED

Mia Torres

Yoga and mobility coach

  • FRC Mobility
