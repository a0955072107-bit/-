Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Give us a call at (555) 012-3456.

Do you charge for a consultation?

Your first 30 minutes are free, in person or by video. If your situation needs deeper analysis or a written opinion, we tell you the fee up front and only start billing once you agree.

How long does it take to form a company?

Once we have everything we need, most companies are registered with the state and set up for taxes in one to two weeks. Foreign ownership or licensed industries can take longer, and we will give you a timeline before we start.

What does monthly bookkeeping cost?

It depends on the size of your business, your monthly transaction volume and whether you need reviewed statements. Small businesses typically start at $250 per month, and we provide a written quote after a short assessment.

I’m not local. Can I still work with you?

Absolutely. We offer video consultations and secure cloud document uploads, and paperwork can be sent by mail. We already work with clients across the country and overseas.

Will my information be kept confidential?

Yes. Our accountants and attorneys are bound by professional confidentiality rules, we sign a confidentiality agreement with every client, and all documents are stored encrypted with access limited to the people working on your file.
