Questions we hear a lot

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp. We usually reply within 30 minutes.

How does the service call fee work?

It’s $89 anywhere in Millbrook. If you hire us for the repair, it’s credited toward the job. If you don’t, that’s all you pay.

Can I send photos for a quote first?

Yes. Send a close-up and a wide shot of the problem on WhatsApp, plus your neighborhood, and we’ll reply with a price range.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Usually under 60 minutes in town. During storms or rush hour we’ll give you a realistic time, and walk you through shutting off the water or power while you wait.

What does the warranty cover?

Our labor is warrantied for one year. If the same problem comes back, we fix it free. Parts carry the manufacturer’s warranty.

Do you work for renters and landlords?

Yes. We recommend checking with your landlord about who pays first, and we can send an itemized invoice straight to them.
