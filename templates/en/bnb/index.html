Small comforts, every day

We’re a family of three running a small inn. Nothing fancy, just good beds, good coffee and a quiet place to rest.

  • Farm breakfast

    Served 8:00–10:00 AM with local eggs, stone-ground grits, biscuits and whatever the market has that week.

  • Cedar hot tub

    Book a private 50-minute soak under the hemlocks. Free for guests, open until 10 PM.

  • Free bikes

    Six cruisers and a kid’s trailer, ready for the greenway down to the river.

  • Dogs welcome

    Up to two dogs under 35 lb in the Garden Room, with a $40 cleaning fee per stay.

  • Free parking

    Six spots right in the driveway. No circling the block.

  • Local know-how

    We’ll set you up with river tubing, orchard picking and the hikes the guidebooks miss.

Easy day trips from the porch

Times are by car, bike or on foot. Leaf season brings weekend traffic, so head out early.

  • Cascade Falls Trail

    An easy mile to a 60-foot waterfall. Bring water shoes in summer.

    20 min drive

  • Big Laurel Loop

    A shady 3-mile loop through old hemlocks and rhododendron tunnels.

    15 min drive

  • Lake Juniper

    Rent a kayak, paddle out to the island and picnic on the dock.

    25 min drive

  • Hot Springs mineral baths

    Soak in warm mineral water by the river, then grab lunch on Main Street.

    40 min drive

Check-in and check-out
  • Check-in is 3:00–9:00 PM. Arriving later? Just let us know.

Deposit and cancellation
  • A 30% deposit within 3 days confirms your booking.
  • Cancel 14 or more days ahead for a full refund.
  • Cancel 7 to 13 days ahead for a 50% refund. Within 7 days, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • Severe weather? Reschedule free or get a full refund.
Parking and pickup
  • Six free spots in the driveway, first come, first served.
  • Free pickup from the downtown bus station; please book a day ahead.
Dogs and kids
  • Dogs stay in the Garden Room only, two at most. Please bring their own bedding.
  • Kids under 6 stay free; we have a crib and kids’ amenities on request.
House rules
  • No smoking anywhere on the property, and no open flames or cooking in rooms.
  • Quiet hours start at 10 PM.
  • We’re a licensed lodging business and can provide receipts.
