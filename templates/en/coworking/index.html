What members say

Questions, answered

Still curious? Give us a call or send a WhatsApp message.

Can I try it before I sign up?

Yes. Book a tour and stay for a free half day at a hot desk. Pick a plan only if you love it.

What's the minimum meeting room booking?

One hour, billed hourly. Book 4 hours or more and save 10%. Members can use their monthly included hours first.

Can I register my company at your address?

Yes. Virtual office and private office plans include a business address you can use for registration, plus mail and package handling.

Do monthly plans require a contract?

Plans run month to month. Commit to 3 months for 5% off, or 12 months for 10% off. Give us 30 days' notice to cancel.

Can I bring guests or my dog?

Guests check in at the front desk and can meet you in the lounge or a meeting room. Well-behaved dogs are welcome on Fridays.
