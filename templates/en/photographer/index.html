How it works

Never been photographed before? No problem. I walk you through every step, so all you have to do is show up as yourself.

  1. Say hello

    Fill out the booking form or send a message. Tell me the mood, date and budget, and I will reply within two days.

  2. Plan together

    We pick the location, outfits and time of day. I scout the light ahead of time and always have a backup plan.

  3. Shoot day

    Less posing, more talking. I give gentle direction without forcing a smile, and we never rush.

  4. Pick and deliver

    Choose favorites in a private online gallery. Final high resolution files arrive by download link, yours to keep forever.

Hi, I’m Nora

I grew up in Cedar Hollow and have been making photographs for ten years. It started with a friend’s wedding; now I photograph around thirty weddings and a hundred or so portrait clients every year.

I love natural light, and I love waiting for it. Waiting for the second a bride finally laughs, or for the sun to land at the end of the street. Great photos aren’t posed. They happen when you relax.
