How it works

First tattoo nerves usually come from not knowing what happens next. Here is the whole thing, start to finish.

  1. Consult

    Send the form or a DM with your idea, placement and rough size. We reply within two days.

  2. Design

    Your artist sketches it up and revises until you love it, then you settle on final size.

  3. Deposit and date

    Pay the deposit to hold your slot. We'll send prep notes: sleep well, eat first.

  4. Tattoo day

    We place the stencil and only start once you're happy. Single-use needles, breaks whenever you want.

  5. Heal and touch up

    Leave with aftercare instructions, message us anytime, and get a free touch-up within three months.

Aftercare

Days 1 to 3
  • Remove the wrap when your artist says to, then wash gently with warm water and fragrance-free soap.
  • Pat dry with a clean paper towel. No rubbing with a cloth towel.
  • Apply a thin layer of aftercare balm and let the skin breathe.
Days 4 to 14
  • Peeling and itching are normal. Don't scratch or pick.
  • Skip baths, pools, hot tubs, saunas and long hours in the sun.
  • Wear loose clothing to cut down on friction.
After the first month
  • Use sunscreen outdoors so lines and color stay crisp for longer.
  • See patchy spots or uneven lines? Book your free touch-up within three months.
When to contact us
  • Spreading redness, heat or pus: see a doctor first, then let us know.
  • Anything else you're unsure about, just send your artist a photo.

Age and health

18+

We only tattoo clients 18 and over. Bring a valid photo ID to your appointment.

  • We don't tattoo during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Tell us at your consult about diabetes, blood clotting issues, heart conditions or a history of keloids.
  • No alcohol in the 24 hours before your session. We can't tattoo anyone who is intoxicated.
  • Sunburn, cuts or rashes on the area need to heal fully before we start.
