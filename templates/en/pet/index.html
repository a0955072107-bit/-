Good to know before you book

A one-minute read that keeps every pet here healthy and safe.

Do you require vaccinations?

Yes. Please bring proof of a current rabies vaccine plus DHPP for dogs or FVRCP for cats. A photo on your first visit is all we need. Puppies and kittens can book once they've had their second round of shots.

What if you find fleas or ticks?

We check every pet before the bath. If we spot fleas or ticks, we'll let you know and switch to a flea treatment bath ($15 extra), then deep-clean the area. Please give a flea and tick preventive at least a week before your visit.

What happens if we're running late?

We hold your spot for 15 minutes. After that we may need to shorten the service or reschedule so the next pup isn't kept waiting. Stuck in traffic? Just send us a WhatsApp message.

How much notice do you need to cancel?

For baths, grooms and daycare, please let us know by 6:00 pm the day before. For boarding, give us 3 days' notice. Holiday boarding requires a deposit, refunded based on how early you cancel.

Can you take senior, sick or anxious pets?

Absolutely. Just tell us about any health issues when you book. We'll build in breaks, keep drying short and may suggest splitting the groom into two visits. Safety always comes first.
