My approach

In my experience, change rarely comes from being talked into it. It tends to grow after you feel understood. In our sessions I follow your pace, and we look together at the feelings and patterns that keep coming back.

  • Person-centered

    You are the expert on your own life. My role is to listen and ask good questions, not hand out answers.

  • Emotion-focused and narrative

    We look at the needs beneath your feelings, and at the story you tell about yourself.

  • Practical next steps

    Coaching sessions are more goal-focused, and each one ends with a small step you can try.

Everyone’s situation and pace are different, and I can’t promise specific results. If we find that another professional or resource would serve you better, I’ll tell you honestly and help with a referral.

Fees, insurance and FAQ

Anything else on your mind? Bring it to the intro call.

How do I pay?

Payment is due at each session by card, HSA/FSA card or bank transfer. For online sessions, your card on file is charged the same day.

Do you take insurance? Can I get a receipt?

I’m an out-of-network provider. I’ll give you a monthly superbill you can submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. Coverage depends on your plan, so check your out-of-network benefits first. Career coaching is not a health service and is not billable to insurance.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead at no charge. Late cancellations are charged half the fee, and missed sessions the full fee.

If you get sick or something urgent comes up, just let me know and we’ll work it out.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. Sessions are kept confidential under professional ethics and privacy law, including HIPAA. I won’t share anything with your family, employer or anyone else. The limits are when someone’s safety is at immediate risk or when the law requires disclosure, and I’ll explain these in our first session.

Online or in person: what’s the difference?

Same care, same fee. Online sessions use a HIPAA-compliant video platform; find a quiet, private spot and use headphones. In-person sessions happen in my soundproofed office, with tea on hand.

Online counseling is available to clients located in states where I’m licensed. If you’re going through a more serious crisis, in-person sessions are usually a better fit.

How long will I need to come?

It varies. Some people find direction in a handful of sessions, others stay longer. We check in regularly, and you can change the frequency or stop whenever you like.

How is counseling different from coaching?

Counseling focuses on emotions, relationships and inner struggles. Coaching focuses on goals, choices and action. We can decide together on the intro call which fits you, or combine the two.
