What we do

Three year-round programs, each one built around what our neighbors actually asked for. We know every kid and every senior by name.

After-school tutoring

Every weekday after school, college students and retired teachers help kids with homework, read together and share a snack.

48 elementary students every week

Senior meal delivery

Our kitchen cooks fresh every morning, and volunteers drive hot lunches to homebound seniors, with a friendly check-in at the door.

70 hot meals every weekday

Family support visits

Our caseworkers visit families at home and help them apply for benefits and find health care and job resources, so no one has to go it alone.

36 families supported year-round

Two hours a week can light up someone's day

No special skills needed, just a willingness to show up regularly. After you sign up, we'll invite you to an orientation and a short training.

  • Homework buddy

    Weekdays 4:00–6:00 PM. Help elementary students with homework and reading. Great for college students and retired teachers.

  • Meal delivery driver

    Weekdays 10:30 AM–12:30 PM. Drive a route of about 10 homes using your own car.

  • Event helper

    Lend a hand at our markets, holiday dinners and fundraisers. Flexible hours.
