How it works

Three steps, each with a record you can read.

  1. 1

    Assessment

    A 40-minute short test and a conversation to see which units need work.

  2. 2

    Learning plan

    The teacher writes down what to cover this semester and in what order. We place your child after you agree.

  3. 3

    Weekly update

    One message a week: what was covered, quiz results and what comes next.

Common questions

Can we try a class first?

Yes. Each class offers one free trial session. We do the assessment first, so the trial is in a class that fits.

What if my child misses a class?

Tell us a day ahead and we will schedule a make-up session the same week, or the teacher will go over that week’s key points separately. Up to two a month.

How do refunds work?

You can withdraw at any time and we refund the unused weeks of the month or term. The full terms are printed on your enrollment form.

Can we sign up for one subject?

Yes. Each subject is its own enrollment. You can add a subject or switch classes mid-semester, and tuition is prorated by the weeks left.
