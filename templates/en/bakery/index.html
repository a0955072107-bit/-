Good to know

How long will bread and cakes keep?

Bread keeps for 2 days at room temperature. Slice and freeze anything extra, then toast straight from frozen. Cream cakes should be refrigerated and eaten within 24 hours; Basque cheesecake keeps 3 days in the fridge.

Do you list allergens?

Most of our products contain gluten, eggs, dairy and nuts, and everything is made in the same kitchen, so we can’t rule out cross-contact. Add a note to your order and we’ll walk you through the ingredients.

Do you ship or deliver?

Cookie and pound cake boxes ship nationwide, with free shipping on orders over $75. Cream cakes and mousses are pickup only to keep them perfect, but we can arrange local delivery within Portland.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can reschedule or cancel for free up to 3 days before pickup. Inside 3 days we’ve already bought ingredients, so the deposit is non-refundable, but you can still move the date once.

How do I pay?

Once we receive your order, we’ll confirm by phone or message and send a payment link. In store we take cash, cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
