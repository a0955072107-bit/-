Your first class

You don’t need to be flexible or experienced. Keep these few things in mind and your first class will feel easy.

  1. Arrive 15 minutes early

    Fill out a short health form, change, and tell your teacher about any tight spots or old injuries.

  2. Skip big meals 2 hours before

    Practice feels better on an empty stomach. If you’re hungry, a banana or a little soy milk is fine.

  3. Follow your own breath

    If a pose doesn’t work today, stop where it feels good. Your teacher will offer a variation, and there’s no need to compare.

  4. Drink warm water afterward

    Your body keeps adjusting after practice. Rest early that night and your shoulders will thank you tomorrow.

What students say

Questions, answered

I don’t exercise at all. Can I still come?

Absolutely. Start with Hatha or Yin, where your teacher will offer props and easier options. Mention it in the booking form and we’ll place you in the right class.

What’s the difference between yoga and reformer Pilates?

Yoga focuses on breath, flexibility and calming the mind. Reformer Pilates uses spring resistance to build deep core strength, which is especially helpful for posture and low back pain. They pair beautifully.

Are classes open to men?

Of course. We have separate changing rooms and showers, and plenty of men take our Vinyasa and reformer classes.

I have an injury or I’m pregnant. What should I know?

Please share the details in the booking form and tell your teacher before class. During pregnancy we recommend Prenatal Yoga, with your doctor’s approval.

Can I share or extend my class pack?

A 10-class pack can be transferred once to a friend or family member, and extended by one month before it expires. Unlimited memberships can be paused once for up to 30 days.

Where do I park?

There’s a free lot behind the building, plus street parking on Juniper Lane. Bus routes 3 and 10 stop two blocks away.
