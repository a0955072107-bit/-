How to book

  1. Message us on WhatsApp

    Tap “Book on WhatsApp” and tell us what you’d like done and which days work for you.

  2. Send photos, pick a time

    Share a photo of your hair now and any inspiration pics. We’ll reply with open times and a price estimate.

  3. A reminder the day before

    We’ll send you a reminder the day before. If something comes up, just let us know on WhatsApp.

  4. Talk it through, then relax

    We start with a 10-minute chat about your routine and what you want. After that, all you have to do is sit back.

Frequently asked questions

What if I’m running late?

Let us know as soon as you can on WhatsApp or by phone. We’ll do our best to hold your spot for up to 15 minutes. After that, we may need to shorten your service or reschedule so the next guest isn’t kept waiting.

How much notice do you need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice. After two same-day cancellations or no-shows, we may ask for a deposit on future bookings. Thanks for understanding.

Is there parking nearby?

There’s metered street parking on Willow Avenue, and a public garage at Willow and 12th, about a 2-minute walk. Spend $150 or more and we’ll validate one hour of parking.

How can I pay?

We take cash, all major credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. For bigger services like color and perms, you can also buy a prepaid package and save a little extra.

It’s my first visit and I’m not sure what style suits me. Is that okay?

Of course! Your stylist will make suggestions based on your face shape, hair type and how you usually style it, and we only start once you’re happy with the plan. No pressure at all.
