Questions

What if I’m running late?

Let us know by WhatsApp or phone as soon as you can. We’ll hold your spot for up to 15 minutes; after that we may need to shorten the service or reschedule so the next guest isn’t kept waiting.

How much notice do you need to cancel or reschedule?

At least 24 hours, please. After two last-minute cancellations or no-shows, we may ask for a deposit on future bookings.

Is there parking nearby?

There’s metered street parking on Willow Avenue and a public garage at Willow and 12th, about a two-minute walk.

How can I pay?

Cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

I don’t know what suits me. Can I still book?

Of course. Your stylist will suggest options based on your face shape, hair texture and how you style day to day, and we only begin once you’re happy with the plan.
