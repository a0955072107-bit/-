A meal that feels like coming home

Hiyori is Japanese for a clear, gentle day. We hope everyone who walks through our door gets to have one here.

In 2016, Chef Kaz and his wife Haru turned the ground floor of an old apartment building into a twelve-table bistro. The menu follows the seasons, the sauces are made in house, desserts come out of the oven every morning, and our beans come from a local roaster we’ve worked with for ten years.

  • Local, seasonal produce

    We buy from a dozen nearby farms and change the menu every season.

  • Single-origin pour-over

    Locally roasted beans, light to dark, rotated daily.

  • Kid and dog friendly

    High chairs inside, and dogs are welcome on the patio.
