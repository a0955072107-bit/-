What the regulars say

Good questions

Do I need an appointment?

Nope. Walk-ins are welcome any time we’re open. If you want a specific barber or you’re coming on a weekend evening, booking ahead saves you the wait.

What if I’m running late?

We hold your chair for 10 minutes. After that we’ll take the next client and fit you into the next open slot. Give us a call if you’re running behind.

How can I pay?

Cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Tips can go on the card too.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, boys 6 and up, at the regular haircut price. First timers are welcome to sit and watch Dad go first.

Where do I park?

There’s metered street parking on Maple and a public garage two minutes away on foot. The Line 15 bus stops right at the corner.
