Questions, answered

Not seeing your question? Call or message us on WhatsApp.

Will servicing here affect my factory warranty?

No. Federal law protects your warranty as long as parts meet factory spec and the work is documented. We give you a dated record every visit.

How long does it take? Can I wait?

An oil service takes about an hour, and our lounge has Wi-Fi and coffee. Coatings and body work need the car overnight; we text you an hour before it is ready.

Can I bring my own oil or parts?

Yes. We warranty the labor but not customer-supplied parts. Send us a photo or part number first so we can confirm the spec.

When can I wash my car after a coating?

Skip washes and rain for the first week. After that, hand wash with a pH-neutral soap and avoid automatic car washes.

What payment do you accept?

Cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Financing is available for coating and body work.
