Before you come

Bring your ID, your insurance card and a list of current medication. There is a short intake form, so please arrive 10 minutes early.

Have a light meal beforehand and wear loose clothing. Skip the tongue scraper that morning, and avoid food or drinks that stain.

Common questions

Do you take insurance?

Some plans cover acupuncture. Send us your member ID and we will check your benefits before your first visit. Herbs and cupping are self-pay, and we tell you the price first.

Does acupuncture hurt?

It differs from person to person. People often describe a dull ache, heaviness or tingling. If you are nervous, tell your practitioner. We can use fewer needles or start with something else.

How do I take the herbs?

Granules are usually mixed with warm water. Follow the directions on the label, and tell us about any medication or supplements you take.

Can I come in while pregnant?

Please tell us how far along you are when you book and again at your visit, so your practitioner can decide what is suitable and what to adjust.
