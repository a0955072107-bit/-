How buying works

First home or fifth, it is five clear steps, and you will never be guessing what comes next.

  1. Talk it through

    We go over budget, down payment and the neighborhoods you love, then run a quick mortgage estimate.

  2. Tour homes

    We line up showings and point out condition, light and HOA fees on site.

  3. Make an offer

    Recent comps in hand, we negotiate a fair price with the seller.

  4. Go under contract

    Inspection, appraisal and title are scheduled, and your earnest money sits safely in escrow.

  5. Close and move in

    We join the final walkthrough, hand over the keys and help set up utilities.
