Your first visit

Three steps, and you can pause to ask questions at any of them.

  1. 1

    Pick a time

    Book on WhatsApp or by phone and tell us what’s bothering you. Bring your insurance card and a photo ID.

  2. 2

    Exam and explanation

    We take only the X-rays we need, then your dentist goes through them with you, along with the options you have.

  3. 3

    Treatment when you’re ready

    You get the cost and number of visits up front. Start when you’re ready; an exam-only visit is fine too.

Treatments

We check your coverage before we start. If something isn’t covered, we explain why and what it costs, and you decide.

  • Checkups and cleanings

    Regular exams and cleanings, with a quick look at anything worth keeping an eye on.

    Usually covered

  • Fillings and root canals

    Fillings for cavities, and root canal treatment when the nerve is inflamed, to keep your own tooth.

    Partly covered

  • Gum care

    Bleeding gums or loose teeth? A periodontal exam and treatment, with regular follow-ups.

    Usually covered

  • Kids’ dentistry

    Checkups, fluoride and sealants. Kids get to know the room before any treatment begins.

    Usually covered

  • Implants and dentures

    Imaging to check bone and overall health, then a clear plan for steps, timing and aftercare.

    Self-pay

  • Braces and aligners

    Consultations for traditional braces and clear aligners, based on how your teeth line up.

    Self-pay

Coverage depends on your plan. We’ll confirm it with you at your visit.
