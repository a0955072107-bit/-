Questions, answered

Don’t see yours here? Just send me an email.

How do you price projects?

Most projects get a fixed price based on scope, so there are no surprise hourly bills. You’ll have an itemized proposal within three business days of our call. Consulting is billed hourly.

How does payment work?

Usually three milestones: 40% to book, 30% when designs are approved and 30% at launch. I invoice by email and accept bank transfer or card.

How many revisions are included?

Two full rounds of design revisions, plus unlimited small copy and image tweaks during the build. Anything new gets quoted and approved before I start on it.

How long does a project take?

A marketing site takes about four to six weeks, product UI about three to five. Rush timelines are possible for an added fee.

Can you do just design or just development?

Absolutely. I often team up with other designers or dev teams, and I’m happy to build from existing designs.
