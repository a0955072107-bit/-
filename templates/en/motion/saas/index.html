Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Call (555) 012-3456 or message us on WhatsApp.

Will I be charged when the trial ends?

No. You don't enter a card for the trial. We'll email you before it ends, and if you don't pick a plan your account pauses and your data is kept for 30 days.

Do clients need to download an app?

No. They open your booking link in any phone browser. You can also add it to Instagram, Google Business or your website.

Can I bring my existing bookings over?

Yes. Use our CSV import template, and on Team plans and above we'll move everything for you, usually within one business day.

Do text reminders cost extra?

Every plan includes a monthly text allowance, and email reminders are unlimited. Extra texts are $0.04 each.

Do you send invoices?

Yes. An invoice is emailed to you every billing cycle, and you can add your business name and tax ID.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans can be canceled anytime and stop billing the next month. Yearly plans can be refunded pro rata.
