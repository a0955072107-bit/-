Before we drew a single wall, we counted the trees

Grove Land Company

28 years building in the region, 14 completed communities

We always start with the land itself. This site had 40 camphor and chinaberry trees, so we pushed the tower north and pulled the basement back to keep every one of them where it stood.

For ten years after you move in, structural and waterproofing issues are ours to fix.

Linden Hale Architects

Principal architect Linden Hale

The facade uses three shades of stone, each floor a little lighter than the one below, so from a distance it reads like a trunk dissolving into sky. Every balcony is five feet deep to keep summer sun out of the living room.

A good building doesn’t need to be the loudest thing on the street. It should make the tree outside your window feel more important than the building itself.

Linden Hale, principal architect

Questions buyers ask us most

How does the payment schedule work?

You pay a deposit at reservation, a second amount at contract, then staged payments as construction milestones are reached, with the balance at closing. Our sales team can run the numbers for any residence.

When will homes be ready?

We expect the certificate of occupancy in the fourth quarter of 2027, with closings over the following three months. Construction updates are posted monthly in the buyer portal.

Can I customize my layout?

Yes. Until the structure tops out you can adjust walls, outlets and selected finishes. The first round of changes is free.

Are deposits protected?

Yes. All deposits are held in a third-party escrow account and released only as construction milestones are certified.

Is there a model home?

A fully furnished Residence B model and a finishes gallery are in the sales center. Viewings run seven days a week, and you can walk the site too.
