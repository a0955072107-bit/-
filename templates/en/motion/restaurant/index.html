Good fire starts with good wood

We cook for twenty-four guests a night. Buying small means we can hand-pick every single thing.

Dawn at the docks

Three mornings a week we drive to Newport before sunrise and buy straight from the boats we know.

A bed of oak coals

Seasoned Oregon white oak burns long, steady and clean. We light the hearth two hours before service.

A pinch of sea salt

Hand-harvested on the Oregon coast, coarse and faintly sweet. It goes on last, right before the plate reaches you.

Chef Mara Quinlan

Mara spent nine years tending wood fires in Basque grill houses, starting as the cook who just kept the coals alive. Back home in Portland, she wanted a room that never rushes: guests at the counter, watching fish skin blister slowly over the embers.

There is no recipe for fire. Every log and every fish is different, so I just keep watching.

Two seatings a night, twelve guests each. Mara hands every course across the counter herself, and she is always happy to tell you where tonight's fish came from.

Before you sit down

Can you work around allergies or things I don't eat?

Absolutely. Add a note when you book and the kitchen will swap in something else from the season. For fully plant-based menus or severe allergies, please give us three days' notice.

Can I bring my own wine?

Please do. Corkage is $35 a bottle, two bottles per party, and we waive one bottle when you add the wine pairing.

Are children welcome?

Counter seats are best for guests 12 and older. Families with younger kids are welcome in the Ash Room, where we can prepare a children's menu.

What happens if we run late?

Each seating is served together, so we hold seats for 15 minutes. Late guests join at the current course, and we are not able to bring back courses already served.

Is there a dress code?

No dress code, just come comfortable. We do ask that you skip strong perfume so everyone can enjoy the aromas from the hearth.
