Our story

Eleven years, and it all started with a borrowed battery.

  1. Fall 2016

    A borrowed battery

    On the photo club's first outing, Sophie's camera died. James lent her a spare battery and said she could give it back next time. She's been giving it back for ten years.

  2. Summer 2018

    Our first road trip

    Halfway down Highway 1, a storm rolled in and we spent two hours in a tiny Big Sur diner. Somewhere between the pie and the thunder, we decided every trip would be a trip for two.

  3. Spring 2021

    A walk-up with a balcony

    Our first home in Oakland came with creaky floors, an orange cat named Mochi and a balcony full of rosemary and basil. Saturdays meant pancakes.

  4. Fall 2025

    A proposal at golden hour

    At sunset in Point Reyes, James pulled out that long-dead battery case. Inside was a ring.

  5. 9.18.2027

    The next chapter, with you

    We're getting married. You've been part of this story, so we'd love for you to be there.

Travel and stay

Coming from out of town? We've arranged shuttles and hotel blocks.

Flying in and shuttles

SFO and Oakland are about an hour away. Shuttles leave the Riverside Lodge downtown at 2:30 and 3:00 PM for a 20-minute ride. Tick the shuttle box on your RSVP so we save you a seat.

Driving

The estate has 120 free parking spaces; just search for Willow Creek Estate. It is wine country, so please take the shuttle or a rideshare if you're drinking. The front desk can call a car.

Attire and gifts

Dress up, but be comfortable. That's the whole rule.

Garden formal at dusk

Suits or a blazer with an open collar are perfect, and ties are optional. Knee-length or floor-length dresses are lovely. The ceremony is on grass, so skip the stilettos, and September evenings dip to about 65°F, so bring a wrap.

  • Sage
  • Dusty blue
  • Ivory
  • Evening navy

Questions

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely. Include them in your party size and add their ages in the note, and we'll set out kids' meals and high chairs.

Can I bring a plus-one?

The names on your invitation are the seats we've saved. Space is limited, so please message us first if you'd like to bring someone. Thanks for understanding.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the conservatory beside the lawn, and the reception is indoors anyway. We'll post an update here by noon on the day.

Can we take photos during the ceremony?

We're having an unplugged ceremony, so please keep phones tucked away and simply be there with us. During cocktails and dinner, snap away.

Who do I ask about anything else?

Our planner Maya is happy to help. Call (555) 012-3456 or send a WhatsApp message, daily 10 AM to 9 PM.
