About Avery

Good motion is not effects. It is timing. The speed a single letter lands at decides how a brand sounds.

After six years as a motion designer at a commercial production house, I opened my own studio in 2020. I now work with a flexible crew of photographers, sound designers and developers, and I stay hands-on from concept and boards to the final frame.

I also teach motion design at a local art college and collect old sign lettering and neon.

How we work

A typical title project runs six to eight weeks. Every stage ends with something you can see, so you always know where things stand.

  1. 01

    Talk

    Goals, audience and where it will play, plus budget and timing. The first call is free.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Two or three directions with mood boards and key frames. We pick one before moving on.

  3. 03

    Make

    Boards, motion tests and sound, with a weekly online screening.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Masters for every platform, source files, a usage guide and one free round of tweaks.
