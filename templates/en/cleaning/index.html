Questions, answered

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not necessarily. We suggest being home for the first visit to walk us through; after that you can leave a key or door code, and we send photos when we finish.

How close is the online quote to the final price?

Usually very close. Your cleaner looks over the space, confirms price and time, and only starts once you agree. There are no surprise charges mid-clean.

Do I need to provide supplies?

No, we bring everything. If you have marble, hardwood or other special surfaces, or want us to use specific products, add a note when you book.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your clean. Cancellations within 24 hours carry a $25 trip fee.

Do you invoice businesses?

Yes. We email receipts for every visit, and office contracts can be billed monthly.
