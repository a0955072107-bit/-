Once your flowers are home

Four small habits that buy most flowers three to five extra days.

  1. 01

    Cut at an angle

    Trim an inch off each stem at a slant, ideally under water, so they can drink more.

  2. 02

    Fresh water daily

    Fill the vase a third of the way, rinse it out and add the flower food we include.

  3. 03

    Keep them cool

    Avoid direct sun, heating vents and the fruit bowl. All three age flowers fast.

  4. 04

    Strip low leaves

    Leaves below the waterline rot quickly. Pull faded petals as you spot them.

From the people who got flowers

Questions, answered

Where do you deliver?

Our own florists deliver across Portland city limits. Beaverton, Lake Oswego, Milwaukie and Gresham are covered too, but not same day. Outside the metro area, choose dried flowers or potted plants and we will ship them overnight.

How much is delivery?

Delivery in Portland is $10, and free on orders over $75. Nearby suburbs are $18. A guaranteed time window adds $8, and overnight shipping is $22.

How long will my flowers last?

Usually 5 to 10 days, depending on the stems. Every bouquet comes with flower food and a care card, and following the tips above helps them last longer. Dried flowers keep for six months or more.

Can I choose specific flowers or colors?

Color stories like “warm orange” or “white and green” are always possible. Specific flowers depend on what we get that week, so add a note to your order and we will call to confirm.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Free changes and cancellations until 6:00 pm the day before delivery. Once we start making your bouquet it can’t be cancelled, but we can still change the address.
