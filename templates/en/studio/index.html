Founded in 2018, Lumen is a small studio of designers and developers. We believe good design doesn’t need to shout.

Research is where we start and restraint is how we work. Every project begins with understanding people: what your audience cares about, and how you want to be remembered.

Then we turn those answers into type, color, rhythm and interaction, so your brand speaks the same language at every touchpoint.
