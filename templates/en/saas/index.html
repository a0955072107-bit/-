Questions, answered

Still unsure? Call (555) 012-3456 Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM, or message us on WhatsApp.

Will I be charged when the trial ends?

No. The trial doesn't need a card. We'll remind you before the 14 days are up, and you only pay if you choose a plan. If you don't, your account pauses and your data is kept for 30 days.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. Clients open your booking link in any phone browser and book with just a name and phone number.

Can I bring over bookings from a spreadsheet or paper diary?

Yes. Import your client list from a spreadsheet in one go and create existing bookings in bulk. On Team and above, our support team can move it for you.

What happens if I run out of text messages?

Top up from your dashboard at $0.04 per message. Top-ups never expire, and WhatsApp and email reminders are unlimited on every plan.

How do deposit refunds work?

Click Refund on the booking and the money goes back to the client's original payment method. Your cancellation rules can decide automatically whether and how much to refund.

Is my client data safe?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest and backed up daily. Staff only see what you give them access to, and you can switch off an account in one click when someone leaves.

Can I cancel or switch plans anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime and you won't be billed again. Upgrades apply right away, and the difference is prorated by day.
