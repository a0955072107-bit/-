Who this summit is for

No big-company transformation theory. Just what a small shop can actually pull off, from people who run shops or help them every day.

01

Storefronts ready to take orders online

Customers keep asking if they can order ahead, and you’re still on the phone with a paper notebook. We’ll help you pick your first tool.

02

Brands stuck at likes, not sales

Posts get hearts but rarely turn into orders. Learn how loyalty texts and short video keep regulars coming back.

03

Long-running shops moving off paper

Twenty years of ledgers and no idea where to start. Hear from owners who switched one step at a time.

04

Next-gen owners taking over the family business

You want to modernize without a fight at the dinner table. Meet owners in the same spot and swap notes.

Eight speakers from the front line

Tap “About the speaker” for full bios. Speakers and talk titles may shift slightly before the day.

Maya Torres

Co-founder, Corner Cup Coffee

How a 400 sq ft café gets 70% of sales from regulars

Maya opened her first café with a college friend in 2016 and grew it to three locations in three years. She swapped paper punch cards for a text-based loyalty club, and regulars now bring in 70% of monthly revenue.

She’ll share the real numbers behind her tiers, message cadence and offers.

Daniel Brooks

Retail advisor, former head of digital at a grocery chain

Going digital with inventory: don’t buy software yet

Daniel spent twelve years digitizing a regional grocery chain before going independent to help small shops. His favorite line: if the process is a mess, every system will be a mess too.

He’ll walk through three real shops and how they started with counts, ordering and reports.

Priya Nair

Social media coach

Sell without chasing virality: short video for local shops

Priya has shot short videos with more than two hundred diners, salons and florists. No dance trends or trending audio, just the owner being themselves on camera.

She also runs an afternoon workshop where you’ll script your first video on your phone.

Sam Whitaker

Second-generation owner, Whitaker Hardware

Three years selling hardware online: five things we got wrong

When Sam took over his dad’s thirty-year-old hardware store, he started selling online. Year one lost money, year two broke even, and by year three online sales matched the store.

Expect a very honest list of expensive lessons.

Grace Kim

E-commerce operations lead

Website, marketplaces or local delivery: where to spend your time

Grace has run online channels for food, gift and skincare brands, and she’s known for simple spreadsheets that show what each channel really earns.

You’ll leave with a channel scorecard to fill in back at the shop.

Marcus Bell

Payments engineer

Card readers, tap to pay and sales tax without the headaches

Marcus spent eight years onboarding merchants at a payments company and has seen every way small shops get stuck on fees, payouts and tax settings.

He’ll compare the common options in plain English and take live questions.

Elena Rossi

Owner, Daily Bloom Flowers

Booking-only florist: one form instead of a ringing phone

Elena runs her flower shop solo and used to field forty calls a day. Since moving to online booking, she finally has time to actually arrange flowers.

In her workshop you’ll build your own booking form on the spot.

Jordan Lee

Data analyst

One spreadsheet to understand your repeat customers

By day Jordan analyzes data for retail brands. By night he teaches shop owners spreadsheets at the community college, because you don’t need expensive tools to understand your business.

Bring a laptop; a template file is provided.

Questions, answered

Still curious? Call (555) 012-3456 weekdays 10 AM to 6 PM, or message us on WhatsApp.

Do workshops cost extra or need a separate sign-up?

No. Every ticket includes workshops. Each seats 40, and doors open 10 minutes before start on a first-come basis.

Can I get an invoice for my business?

Yes. Add your business name and address in the notes when you register, and we’ll email an invoice within 7 days after the event.

What if I can’t make it?

Full refund up to 14 days before, 80% between 7 and 13 days before, and no refunds after that. You can always transfer your ticket to a colleague or friend for free.

Will sessions be recorded?

Main-stage sessions are recorded and sent to every attendee within two weeks. Slides that speakers agree to share go out the same evening.

Is lunch included? Are there vegetarian options?

Yes. Every ticket includes a boxed lunch and afternoon coffee, and you can pick vegetarian or vegan on the form.

Can I bring my kids or a stroller?

Yes. The venue has elevators and a nursing room. Children under 12 attend free with an adult but don’t get a reserved seat.
