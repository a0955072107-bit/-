How we work

Six steps, each with written notes and a clear timeline, so you always know where your project stands.

  1. Consult

    We talk about who lives there, how you use the space and what you want to spend.

    Free, about 60 minutes

  2. Measure

    We measure and photograph the site and check for leaks, wiring and structural issues.

    Free in Portland, within a week

  3. Propose

    Floor plans, 3D renderings and a first estimate, revised until it feels right.

    About 2 to 3 weeks

  4. Sign

    Final drawings, a line-item scope and payment milestones, all in writing.

    Paid in stages

  5. Build

    Your designer supervises on site and sends progress photos and notes every week.

    About 6 to 14 weeks

  6. Handover

    A room-by-room walkthrough, a deep clean, your warranty and a care guide.

    2-year warranty

What clients say

Questions, answered

Don’t see yours? Call or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll help.

How much should I budget?

For an older Portland home, a partial refresh usually runs $90 to $140 per sq ft, and a full remodel with new plumbing and wiring $180 to $260 per sq ft. After we measure, you get a line-item quote so you can adjust finishes and scope to fit.

How long from first call to move-in?

Design takes about 4 to 6 weeks and construction 6 to 14 weeks, depending on size and scope. For a full 1,000 sq ft remodel, plan on roughly four months.

Is the work under warranty?

Yes. Millwork, cabinetry, paint, plumbing and electrical carry a 2-year warranty, and waterproofing carries 5 years. If anything comes up, we’ll be on site within 3 business days.

What should I know about renovating an older home?

In homes over 30 years old we recommend updating plumbing and wiring and sealing against water. We test moisture and electrical safety when we measure, so the hidden things get fixed before the pretty ones.

Do you charge for the site visit?

Site visits inside Portland city limits are free. Elsewhere in the metro area there’s a $75 travel fee, credited in full toward your design fee when you sign.

Can you refresh part of a shop or office?

Absolutely. For retail, restaurants and clinics we schedule work on evenings and weekends where possible, so you stay open for business.
