How we teach

More hours in a classroom is not the goal. Making sure every lesson actually lands is.

Small groups

6 to 12 students per class, so teachers know every name and can see how each student works through a problem.

Monthly progress reports

Skill by skill progress, homework completion and teacher notes, sent to parents at the start of each month.

Recorded review lessons

Key explanations from every class are recorded, so students can catch up after an absence or review before a test.

Placement check first

A 30 minute diagnostic shows which skills need work before we recommend a class.

Homework room

Open from 2:00 pm on weekdays, with a teacher on hand to help. Homework gets done before students head home.

Easy to reach us

Parents get a message when students arrive and leave, and can text their child's teacher with any question.

Frequently asked questions

Is the trial class really free? How many can we try?

Each student can try one class per subject for free. We start with a short placement check, and afterwards the teacher talks you through what they noticed.

How big are the classes? Are students grouped by level?

Elementary and enrichment classes have 6 to 10 students, and middle and high school classes have 10 to 12. Within a grade, students are placed in a foundations or advanced group based on the placement check.

What happens if my child misses a class?

Let us know before class by text or WhatsApp. Your child can watch the recorded review, or book time in the homework room so a teacher can help them catch up.

What is in the monthly progress report?

Progress on each skill, homework completion, quiz scores and a note from the teacher, plus the focus for the coming month.

How does billing work? Can we cancel?

Tuition is billed monthly by card or bank transfer. You can cancel with 30 days' notice, and we share the full refund policy in writing when you enroll.

Do kids need their own laptop for coding classes?

No. Every student gets a computer and a robotics kit in class. If your child wants to practice at home, the teacher will help you set up a free practice environment.
