# Make a website template look like your own shop in one afternoon

*Subtitle: Colours, text, photos, map and contact form: the five changes that turn a template into your business.*

The worry people have about templates is that their site will look like everyone else's. In practice, five changes are enough to make a template feel like yours. None of them need coding experience, just a text editor and an afternoon.

## 1. Your colour

Our templates keep the brand colour in one place at the top of the file:

```css
:root {
  --brand: #b4532a;
}
```

Change that one value to your colour, from your logo, sign or menu, and every button, link and highlight on the page follows. If you don't have a brand colour, pick the main colour of your storefront.

## 2. Your words

Replace the sample text with your own, section by section. Two tips:

- **Write how you talk to customers.** "We've been fixing smiles in Arcadia since 1995" beats "We provide comprehensive dental solutions."
- **Keep it short.** One or two sentences per section is plenty on a phone.

If your site has several languages, update English first, then the Chinese pages. Each language is its own file, so you can edit the Chinese menu without touching the English one.

## 3. Your photos

Swap the sample images for real ones: your storefront, your team, your work. Daylight phone photos are fine. Resize them to around 1600 pixels wide before adding them, so the page stays fast on mobile data.

## 4. Your map and phone

Find your shop on Google Maps, tap **Share**, copy the link, and paste it into the "Get directions" button. Put your phone number in the call button in the `tel:` format, for example `tel:+16265550123`, so it dials with one tap.

## 5. Your contact form

A contact form doesn't need a server. A service like Formspree has a free plan that emails form submissions straight to your inbox. Sign up, copy your form address, and paste it into the form's `action` in the template.

## Then check it on your phone

Open the page on your phone before publishing. Tap every button: call, map, language switch, form. That's what your customers will do.
