# Your Google Business Profile matters more than your website

*Subtitle: Most customers meet your business on Google Maps first. Here's how to make that first impression count, and how your website fits in.*

When someone searches "dentist near me" or "dumpling restaurant Flushing", the first thing they see usually isn't a website. It's a box on Google Maps with your name, stars, hours, photos and a few buttons. That's your Google Business Profile, and for most local shops it gets far more views than the website does.

The good news: it's free, and you can fix most of it in an afternoon.

## Five things to fill in properly

1. **Hours, including holidays.** Use "special hours" for Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year and Lunar New Year. Nothing loses a customer faster than a closed door when Google said "open".
2. **Your website link.** If you have a site, link it. If you have several languages, link the main page; visitors can switch from there.
3. **Photos.** At least ten: the storefront (so people recognise it), the inside, and your work or your dishes. Phone photos in daylight are fine.
4. **Services or menu, with price ranges.** People want a rough idea before they call.
5. **Replies to reviews.** Thank people for good ones. Answer bad ones calmly, apologise, and invite them to contact you directly. Future customers read your replies.

## Keep your name the same everywhere

If your sign says "Golden Leaf Dumpling House / 金葉餃子館", use the same name on Google, on your website and on Yelp. Mismatched names, especially between English and Chinese versions, make it harder for Google to connect them and harder for customers to trust they've found the right place.

## So why have a website at all?

Because the profile is limited. It can't explain your treatments, show a full menu in three languages, take a pre-order or tell your story. The profile gets people's attention; the website answers the questions that make them choose you.

The two work best together:

- The profile links to the website.
- The website shows the same name, address, phone and hours as the profile.
- The website has a "Get directions" button that opens the same Google Maps listing.

## A five-minute check

Search your business name on your phone right now. Are the hours right for this week? Is the website link there? Is the most recent review answered? If any answer is no, that's your next five minutes.
