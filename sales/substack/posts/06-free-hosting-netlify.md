# Put your business website online for free in 10 minutes

*Subtitle: A step-by-step guide to Netlify Drop: no account fees, HTTPS included, and your own domain when you're ready.*

If your website is a few HTML files, a folder with an `index.html` and some images, you don't need to pay anyone to host it. Several services host static sites for free. The simplest is Netlify Drop, where you literally drag a folder onto a web page.

Here's the whole process.

## Before you start

You need a folder that contains an `index.html` file. If you bought one of our templates, that's the template folder after you've edited your text. Open `index.html` in your browser first and check that it looks right on your computer.

## Step 1: Drag the folder onto Netlify Drop

1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag your whole folder (not the files inside it, the folder itself) onto the page.
3. Wait a few seconds. Netlify gives you a random address like `cheerful-dumpling-12ab34.netlify.app`.

Your site is now online, with HTTPS.

## Step 2: Create a free account so the site stays up

Sites dropped without an account are temporary. Click the prompt to sign up (email or GitHub is fine) and claim the site. It's free for a site like this, and there's no card required.

## Step 3: Give it a readable name

In the site settings, change the site name, for example to `golden-leaf-dumpling`. Your address becomes `golden-leaf-dumpling.netlify.app`. That's already good enough to put on a Google Business Profile or a business card.

## Step 4 (optional): Use your own domain

When you want `goldenleafdumpling.com` instead:

1. Buy the domain from a registrar such as Cloudflare, Namecheap or Porkbun, in your own name.
2. In Netlify, open **Domain management → Add a domain** and type it in.
3. Netlify shows you the DNS records to add. Copy them into your registrar's DNS settings.
4. Wait a little while for DNS to update. Netlify then adds HTTPS automatically.

## Updating the site later

Edit the files on your computer, then drag the folder onto the **Deploys** tab of your site. The new version replaces the old one in seconds. Nothing to log into except Netlify, nothing to update, no plugins.

## What this costs

Hosting: $0. Domain: around $10–15 a year. That's it.
