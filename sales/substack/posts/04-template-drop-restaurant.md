# New template: a restaurant site that knows when you're open

*Subtitle: Menu tabs, a live open/closed badge and a reservation form, in four languages.*

The most-visited part of any restaurant website is the opening hours. So the first thing on this template is a small badge that reads the current time and says "Open now, until 9:30 PM" or "Closed now, opens tomorrow at 11:00 AM." Change your hours in one place and the badge, the hours table and Google's structured data all update together.

👉 [Live demo](https://a0955072107-bit.github.io/-/templates/restaurant/en/) · tap the language button to switch to 中文, 日本語 or Español.

## What's in it

- **Live open/closed badge** based on your real hours, in your time zone
- **Tabbed menu** (lunch, dinner, drinks) that also works with JavaScript off
- **Reservation form** that emails you, via a free Formspree account
- **Sticky "Book a table" and "Call" buttons** on phones
- **Map and directions** link, without embedding a tracking map
- **Five languages** with a one-tap switcher: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Spanish
- **Google-ready**: structured data for your name, address, hours and price range

## Who it's for

Cafés, neighbourhood restaurants, noodle shops, bakeries with seating. Anywhere people mostly want to know: are you open, what's on the menu, and how do I book.

## How it works after you buy

1. Download the zip and open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Search for the sample name, phone and address and type your own.
3. Change the colours in one place at the top of the file.
4. Drag the folder onto Netlify. You're live, free.

The guide inside walks through each step, in English, 中文, 日本語 and Español.
